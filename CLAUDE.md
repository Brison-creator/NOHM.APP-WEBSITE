# nohm.app website

Static site. Amplify serves `public/` as-is (see `amplify.yml`); there is no build step.

## Rules for every page

- **Layout copies tesla.com.** Homepage: full-screen photo carousel with the title and the App Store and Google Play badges (with a quiet Learn More link) over the photo, the bar clear over the photo until you scroll (then white), gray cards below, and on phones the visitor's store badge pinned to the bottom beside the support chat.
- **Keep explanations short, Tesla style:** a photo, a short title, one line. Use the feature row (`/features.css` + `/features.js`) for that; lists become short spec lists.
- Homepage trades: each trade gets a full-screen `<section class="panel">` near the bottom of the homepage (Lawn Care was removed for launch: recurring, quoted work doesn't fit Standard/Express/NOHM NOW yet; photo kept at public/img/lawn-*). Paint out third-party logos (e.g. mower, truck brands) before publishing photos.
- Services: the homepage shows 8 featured cards and a **Browse All Services** button to `/services`, which lists all 15 trades (Plumbing, HVAC, Electrical, Appliance, Locksmith, Pressure Washing, Handyman, Landscaping & Lawn Care, Gutter Service, Garage Door Service, Pest Control, Painting, Flooring, House Cleaning, Tree Service). The homeowners page tiles carry the same 15. Add or rename a trade in all three places.
- A service card can play a short loop instead of a photo: a silent 720px `public/video/<trade>-720.mp4` + `.webm` as a `<video>` after the card's `<img>`, with the photo as its poster (Plumbing, Appliance and Locksmith do this on the homepage and `/services`, Landscaping, Pest Control, Painting and Flooring on `/services`; set `object-position` on a card's video to keep the pro in frame).
- The bottom two homepage sections (the card row and For Local Pros) have a gentle depth effect: each photo overhangs its frame by 8% top and bottom and drifts slower than the page (script at the end of `index.html`; off with reduced motion). A new card or panel photo gets it automatically.
- The family at the chess board ("Home is for living.") is a looping video, `public/video/family-*`, on the homepage card, the `/homeowners` hero and the For Pros feature row; its photo (`public/img/home-*`) is kept but no longer used.
- The "One Standard" card (homepage) and "Any Pro, Same Standard" card (`/local-pros`) play `public/video/standard-*` (Diaz Plumbing and Express Plumbing); the old Mike's/Ray's photo (`public/img/standard-*`) is kept but no longer used.
- The homepage's "NOHM is advanced." section plays `public/video/advanced-*`, rendered from `tools/space-zoom/space-zoom.html` (not served). To change it: edit that page (open it in a browser to preview), serve the repo root, run `node tools/space-zoom/render.mjs out.mp4 1080 1080 ""` (FFMPEG and PLAYWRIGHT env point at the binaries), then encode 1080 and 720 MP4 + WebM and a poster like the other videos.
- Service photos: drop a photo at `public/img/services/<trade>.jpg` and it fills that card on the homepage and `/services`; empty cards show a faint icon. Names: plumbing, hvac, electrical, appliance, locksmith, pressure-washing, handyman, landscaping, gutters, garage-door, pest-control, painting, flooring, house-cleaning, tree-service.
- Videos live in `public/video/`: silent, looping, with an MP4 (H.264, +faststart) and WebM (VP9) at 720px for phones and full size for desktop, plus a poster JPG. Strip audio. Hero slides are stills that come to life once: `<video class="once" muted playsinline>` (no loop, no autoplay) plays from the start when its slide becomes current and holds on its last frame.
- Spacing matches tesla.com on phones: cards inset 12px from the screen edge with 12px corners, 24px between stacked cards, text 24px from the edge (16px inside a card), 64px under a text section's last line, sliding cards 16px apart with ~17px of the next card peeking.
- `/dispatch` is the Verified Dispatch Engine animation (one canvas, no libraries, not linked from the menu yet). It is the one page with its own dark palette (obsidian `#05070B`, emerald `#00E676`, amber `#FFB300`), set as tokens at the top of its file; don't carry those colors to other pages.
- `/how-it-works` is a playable demo of the app (`public/how-it-works/demo.js`, `demo.css`): every role on sample data in the browser, login `test@nohm.app` / `password`. It never talks to the NOHM server, so that login must never exist on the real one. When the app's flows or rules change (for example the 30-minute card pause), change the demo to match. Fees come from `/pricing.js`.
- `/try` and `/booth` are the trade show demo, built on the same `demo.js`, and deliberately **not in the menu** and **without the site's top bar** (they are the app, full screen):
  - `/try` is where the booth's QR code points (`public/booth/qr-try.svg`, print file `qr-try-print.png`; regenerate with `tools/booth/make_qr.py`). The app edge to edge on a phone, login filled in, "Get the app" when a job is done.
  - `/booth` runs on the booth iPad or TV: homeowner and pro side by side on one shared job. With no touches it plays itself (a Standard repair, then an Express job with a declined card that pauses and is fixed); any touch hands it over clean; a minute idle brings the loop back. It works offline once loaded (`public/booth/sw.js`: bump `CACHE` when its file list changes) and installs to a home screen (`manifest.webmanifest`, icons from `tools/booth/make-icons.mjs`). `?fast` plays the loop quickly for testing.
- `/landlords` and `/renters` use the homeowners page template and describe the rentals features of the NOHM 1.1 app (`Brison-creator/the-nohm-application-1.1`): rent by ACH straight to the landlord's connected payout account (NOHM takes no percentage of rent; ACH collection is 0.8%, max $7, with a per-lease landlord choice to pass it to the tenant or absorb it), leases e-signed, condition reports and NOHM Walkthroughs, deposit returns, Money & Taxes per unit (Schedule E), listings and applications, NOHM Manager (hands-off), and repairs. Rental prices live in `pricing.js` under `rentals`: $9/month membership per landlord account + $7/rental/month, Manager $29/managed rental/month, Walkthrough $79, and applicant screening $40 Standard / $50 Instant Income / $65 Premium where local law permits. The site shows customer-facing prices only—never NOHM margin or provider wholesale cost. Screening uses Certn for core/source employment checks and Plaid for bank-connected income. `public/landlords/pricing.css` owns only the landlord pricing/comparison component. Competitor figures on `/landlords` must link the provider's own current pricing/help page and state when they were checked. Zillow/Trulia/HotPads syndication is built but waiting on Zillow's approval, so it says "coming soon" until it's switched on.
- `/join` (pros) and `/join/renter` (renters) are the **web sign-up flows**: the app's sign-up with `role: CONTRACTOR` or `TENANT`, then the role's onboarding against the server (pro: profile + trades, documents, Stripe Connect payouts, the NOHM Standard, submit for review; renter: the landlord's 6-digit invite + a text code). Rules in `public/join/lib/*-flow.js`, screens in `public/join/ui/`, the client shared with `/book` in `public/nohm/`. The server's dashboard decides where a pro is; the site stores no progress. The Standard list on the agreement screen is the one on `/local-pros`; change both. Run `node --test tools/join/*.test.mjs` and the headless run in `docs/web-join.md` before pushing.
- **"I'm human" and the security marks** on `/book`, `/join` and `/join/renter`: every form that sends a text or email code (sign-up, sign-in by code, password sign-in, a new code) shows Cloudflare Turnstile's box (`public/nohm/human.js`) once the server's `GET /config/web` gives `turnstileSiteKey`, and sends its token as `humanToken`; nothing is sent until it's ticked. The server checks it (backend `common/human-check`). Under the account and card steps, `public/nohm/trust.js` shows what protects the visitor: Encrypted (HTTPS), Human-verified (only when the box is on), Payments by Stripe (card step only), Never sold (the Privacy Policy says NOHM doesn't sell personal information). Only true statements: no "bank-level", no certifications NOHM doesn't hold, no third-party logos besides the ones already allowed.
- `/book` is the **web booking flow**: a homeowner requests a pro from the site against the real NOHM server (`https://api.nohm.app/api/v1` in `public/book/config.js`; the Stripe publishable key and Google client id come from the server's `GET /config/web`, never kept in the site), with the same endpoints and rules as the app. Rules live in `public/book/lib/flow.js`, screens in `ui/screens.js`, the server client (http, session, endpoints, sign-up, account screen) in `public/nohm/`; the page never decides price, access or card-on-file (the server does), never uses innerHTML, and honors `?api=` only on localhost. The bookable trades are whatever `GET /trades` returns with `bookable`; the issue list is a copy of the app's `service_issues.dart`, change both. `ui/screens.js` has an icon (`TRADE_ICON`, one 24px stroke path, drawn from the `/services` card) and the `/services` card's words (`BLURB`) for every service; a tile still appears only once the server returns the trade, so a new service is a server trade (`backend/prisma/trades/catalog.ts` in the app repo) plus these and its issues, never a tile the site adds itself (`tools/book/trades.test.mjs` checks them against `/services`). Before pushing a change under `public/book`, run `node --test tools/book/*.test.mjs` and the headless run in `docs/web-booking.md`. The four bookable cards on the homepage and `/services` link to `/book?trade=<slug>`. It needs the server's `CORS_ORIGIN` to include `https://nohm.app`.
- Every page loads `/menu.js`, which puts the **Menu** button in the bar and builds the full-screen menu. New pages go in its list.
- Every indexable page's `<head>` carries a canonical (`https://nohm.app/<path>/`, trailing slash), Open Graph tags (title and description copied from `<title>` and the description meta, `og:image` `/img/crew-1672.jpg`) and `twitter:card`, and is listed in `public/sitemap.xml` (`/try` and `/dispatch` are left out on purpose). Noindex pages (`/booth`, `/stripe/*`, `404.html`) get none of these. `public/404.html` is the not-found page; keep its links root-relative. Run `node --test tools/site/*.test.mjs` (tags, sitemap, robots, every internal link) before pushing.

- **Design follows Tesla / Starlink:** white background, charcoal body text `#393c41`, near-black headings `#171a20`, 4px corners, lots of space.
- **Every page carries the top bar.** Link `/bar.css` in the head and open `<body>` with:
  ```html
  <header class="bar"><a href="/">NOHM</a></header>
  ```
  Also set `<meta name="theme-color" content="#ffffff" />` so the phone's status bar matches.
- **Typography lives in `/type.css`**, which every page links first (after preloading the two font files). Use its tokens, never a literal font stack or weight:
  - `--font-display` **Sora**: hero and major headlines 800 (`--weight-hero`), section headings 700 (`--weight-heading`), tracking `--tracking-headline` (-0.04em) plus `--word-spacing-headline`; small titles use `--tracking-title`.
  - `--font-text` **Manrope**: body 400/500 (`--weight-body`, `--weight-body-strong`), navigation, buttons, forms and labels 600/700 (`--weight-ui`, `--weight-ui-strong`), normal tracking. Button labels use `--size-button`.
  - `h1`–`h4` pick up Sora by default; for anything else use `.t-hero`, `.t-heading`, `.t-title`, `.t-body`, `.t-lede`, `.t-ui`.
  - The fonts are self-hosted WOFF2 in `public/fonts/` (official google/fonts builds, cut to the weights above and Latin), with their SIL OFL licenses beside them. A new weight means rebuilding the file, not adding a CDN link.
  - The bar's "NOHM" is Sora 800, -0.04em, `#171a20` on the white bar (white only while the homepage bar is clear over its photo). It is the same wordmark as the app's sign-in screen and the admin panel; don't restyle it on one without the others.
  - **NOHM™**: every wordmark carries a small raised ™ (the owner's trademark notice). On the bar it comes from `bar.css` (`.bar > a:first-child::after`), in the app demos from `.d-mark` / `.k-mark`; never type it into the markup, and keep it out of screen readers (the CSS alt text does). Footers read `NOHM&trade; &copy; 2026`. Running text says plain "NOHM".
- **"NOHM it." is a protected brand treatment**: the homepage's `h1.mark`, in **Panchang** 600 with its own size and spacing. Don't change it, and don't use Panchang for anything else. Panchang loads from Fontshare on the homepage only
  (`https://api.fontshare.com/v2/css?f[]=panchang@600&display=swap`); never commit its files, since the ITF Free Font License forbids redistributing them and this repo is public.
- Palette is white, black and NOHM blue, nothing else:
  - background `#ffffff`; top bar white with a `#e6e7e9` hairline (the homepage's is clear over its photo until you scroll); `theme-color` `#ffffff`
  - headlines and the wordmark `#171a20`; body text `#393c41`; small print `#6e6e73` (5.1:1 on white; `#86868b` only for disabled controls)
  - action buttons are NOHM blue: `#356CA3` with white text (hover `#2b5885`), 4px corners, the same as the app and the admin panel. The second button in a pair is white with `#393c41` text and a `#d0d1d2` border. One blue button per group. Downloads are never a button: they use the store badges (below)
  - otherwise NOHM blue `#356CA3` is for the tagline and links inside text
- NOHM only steps in on no-shows/abandoned jobs, unsafe or inappropriate conduct, and unapproved charges; everything else is between homeowner and pro, but reports count toward a pro's standing (like rideshare). Don't promise refunds or guarantees beyond that. See /step-in.
- Never say good work earns pros more jobs; meeting the NOHM Standard keeps them in good standing.
- Copy about pros and the NOHM Standard comes from the NOHM Marketing Plan (section 5); don't invent policies.
- Never use "Keep money local" or anything about it, anywhere on the site.
- Marketing pages (homepage, `/homeowners`) link `/screens.css` for the shared screen template and footer.
- **Every download is the store badges**, never a "Download Now" / "Get NOHM" text button: the `.store-cta` component in `/store-cta.css` (markup in the file's header comment; link the file from any page that uses it). Apple's black "Download on the App Store" and Google's "Get it on Google Play" sit side by side at one height (48px, 44px on phones), with an optional `a.store-cta__more` text link for the page's secondary action; add `store-cta--center` and/or `store-cta--on-dark` (over photos and dark panels). The artwork in `public/img/badges/` is Apple's and Google's official files, untouched (Google's only cropped to its visible edge): never recolor, outline or redraw them, and keep a quarter of the badge height clear around each. Where only one badge fits (the homepage's phone dock), wrap it in `.store-pick`: `/menu.js` marks Android phones with `<html data-platform="android">` and they get Google Play, everyone else the App Store.
- Every page's `<head>` links the site icon right after `theme-color`: `/favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `/icon-192.png`, `/icon-512.png` and `/apple-touch-icon.png` (180), made from the app's black-and-white icon (white house and power glyph on black), opaque. `/booth` and `/try` keep their own NOHM wordmark icons.
- NOHM is free to use: Standard (a pro in a day or two) has no NOHM fee; Express is same day; NOHM NOW is within 60 minutes of the pro accepting; pros choose Ready Now and go live for four hours, ready to leave immediately. NOHM fees shown on the site come from `/pricing.js`. Launch pricing is on (Express $20, NOHM NOW $30) until 1,000 activated homeowners; then set `launch: false` there (regular $40 / $60). The app's own admin setting is separate.
- Never list coverage areas or towns. Coverage follows contractor onboarding: say NOHM is "available wherever local pros have joined" and grows as pros sign on.
- Each page lives at `public/<path>/index.html` so clean URLs like `/privacy` work.
- Three Quotes (`/quotes`, the pros page "Three Quotes" section, the homeowners FAQ): copy must match the Three Quotes build. Launch categories are replacements only (HVAC system, packaged HVAC unit, water heater, tankless water heater, electrical panel, standby generator). Payment rule: the pro enters the complete customer price and that is the homeowner's total ($1,000 quote = $1,000 total; use the $1,000 / $50 / $950 example everywhere). The homeowner pays a 5% deposit through NOHM, held on the contractor's behalf and credited toward the accepted quote, and pays the remaining 95% under the contractor's terms. The contractor pays NOHM a 5% service fee, deducted from the funds collected for the contractor; it is never added to the homeowner's price. Don't say NOHM "keeps" the homeowner's deposit. Never say "marketplace", and never name a county or coverage area. Exception, NOHM Financing: when the homeowner pays over time with Affirm, NOHM collects the whole price through Affirm and the 5% is inside it, taken once (no separate deposit).
- **NOHM Financing (`/financing`)**: homeowners can pay over time with Affirm on eligible projects; Affirm decides on its own page, NOHM and pros never take credit applications, and NOHM adds no surcharge. Never write an APR, "0%", a monthly payment or an approval promise of our own (Stripe's Affirm messaging element may show Affirm's own). Keep Affirm's lender disclosure word for word under any Affirm mention. For pros it is an option after joining (never part of sign-up), listed on their profile as "Financing with Affirm"; a pro's own lender is "alternative financing outside the NOHM network". Affirm's logo is its official file, untouched, in `public/img/logos/affirm.svg` (black wordmark, blue arc, white ground): only on white, never recolored or redrawn, like the store badges. Stripe's wordmark is the same path as on `/quotes`.
