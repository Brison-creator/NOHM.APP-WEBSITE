Text Messages from NOHM

NOHM LLC texts people from 888-230-NOHM (888-230-6646) about their own NOHM account, jobs and rentals. They are transactional only. NOHM does not send marketing, promotional or referral texts.

What we text

  • Login codes, for example: "Your NOHM verification code is: 482913. Valid for 5 minutes."
  • Job updates for homeowners, for example: "NOHM: Your pro is about 5 minutes away. Open the NOHM app to follow them on the map and have your arrival PIN ready."
  • New work orders for pros, for example: "New NOHM work order. A homeowner chose you. Open the app to review the details and send them a message. Thanks for taking great care of our local homeowners."
  • Rental and lease steps: a tenant's invite code from their landlord, an application link the applicant asked for, application decisions, move-in payment links and receipts, and lease codes. For example: "Your landlord invited you to NOHM. Your verification code is: 7K2Q9D. Open the NOHM app to accept. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
  • Emergency alerts to a landlord about a property they manage, for example: "NOHM EMERGENCY: Water leak at 104 Oak St, reported 2:14 PM. Open the NOHM app to see the Emergency Card and get help."
  • Notices about charges on your account, such as a cancellation fee

The first text NOHM sends a number that hasn't signed up ends with "Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help." NOHM never texts gate codes, door codes or other access details.

How you agree to texts

1. Creating a NOHM account. You give us your mobile number when you create a NOHM account in the NOHM app. Right under the phone number field, the sign-up screen says:

By entering your number, you agree to get account texts from NOHM, like login codes and job updates. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

The same screen asks you to agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before your account is created, and your number is confirmed with a one-time code.

2. Applying for a rental on a NOHM listing. You type your own mobile number into the Apply form, which says:

By applying you agree to get texts from NOHM about this application. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

3. Your landlord sending you an invite or an application. A landlord can send a tenant they have a lease with, or an applicant who asked them for an application, one text with an invite code or an application link. The app keeps Send off until the landlord confirms:

They asked for this and agreed to get a text from NOHM about it. Msg & data rates may apply. They can reply STOP to opt out.

Invitations to join NOHM as a pro are never texted from NOHM's number. The person inviting sends them from their own phone.

How often

Message frequency varies with your account activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping texts, and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any NOHM text to stop getting texts. We confirm once and send nothing more.
  • Reply START to get texts again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at support@nohm.app or 888-230-NOHM (888-230-6646).

Agreeing to texts is not a condition of any purchase. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your number stays private

Opt-in data and consent for SMS messaging will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
