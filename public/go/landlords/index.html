The facts

Cost
Free for the first 10,000 landlords (NOHM Founding Partners), for good. No plan, no monthly fee.
Rent
Tenants pay by bank, straight to your account. NOHM takes no part of the rent. Each bank payment has a $7 fee: the tenant pays it, or you can.
Repairs
Tenants send repairs with photos. You approve, and a NOHM pro near the rental does the work. You see the price before you book, and pay only for work you book.
Your contractors
Invite the handyman, plumber or electrician you already use, right from the app. After their first job at a rental, make them its go-to pro, and its next repairs of that kind go to them first.
Leases
Signed in the app and kept there.
Applications
Applicants pay $50 for their background check, where the law allows.
Records
Every repair and its service report, kept for each rental and each unit.
Money
Income, costs and profit for each unit, and a tax report for your accountant.
Autopilot
Optional, $19.99 a month per rental. It answers your tenants and handles repairs up to a limit you set.
Card on file
Needed to add a rental or invite a contractor, to keep fake accounts out. It’s charged only for work you book.
Ads
None. No spam.
Where
iPhone, Android, and a computer at business.nohm.app.

How to start

  1. Sign up free.
