Rent
Tenants pay by bank, straight to your account. NOHM takes no part of the rent. Each bank payment has a $7 fee: the tenant pays it, or you can. About $5 of it is the bank processor’s cost, and NOHM keeps about $2.

Card on file

Questions landlords ask.

Does NOHM take a cut of my rent?

No. Rent goes straight to your connected payout account. Your tenant pays the bank payment fee, or you can cover it for them.

Why is it free?

NOHM is free for the first 10,000 landlords as a launch deal. NOHM earns a small part of each bank payment fee (about $2 of the $7), the background check applicants pay for, and the repairs and walkthroughs you choose to book. Autopilot is optional.

Do I have to put a card on file?

Yes, you add one when you add a rental or invite a contractor. It’s kept only to pay for repairs you approve and to keep fake accounts out. It’s charged only for work you book, never as a subscription.

What if I’m not in the first 10,000?

You’re on the plans as priced. See them on the landlords page.

What does Autopilot do?

It answers tenant messages from your house notes, books repairs, reviews every estimate, relists the unit after move-out and sends you a weekly summary. Emergencies always come straight to you. It’s optional.

Are the notices and forms legal advice?

No. They’re tools to help you get the paperwork done. See the forms by state.
