Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every rental gets everything: listings, applications, tenant invites, leases, repairs, messages, rent collection, the books and Autopilot. No plan, no card, no monthly fee. If you book a pro for a repair or a NOHM walkthrough, you pay for that work, and you see the price before you book.

So how does NOHM make money?

Two ways, and you pay neither. When your tenant pays rent by bank, they pay a $7 fee: about $5 is the bank processor’s cost, and NOHM keeps about $2. And applicants pay for their background check, where the law allows. No spam, no ads.

Does NOHM take a cut of my rent?

No. Tenants pay by bank, straight to your account. The $7 bank payment fee is your tenant’s, not yours.

Can I bring the tenants I already have?

Yes. Invite them from the app. They accept on their phone, or on the web at nohm.app, and you’re connected.

Who fixes things?

Your tenant sends the repair with photos, and you approve it with a tap. It goes to a local NOHM pro, and you both see it happen. NOHM is available wherever local pros have joined, and grows as more sign on.

What does Autopilot do?

It answers your tenants and handles repairs within the limit you set, so you’re basically 90% hands-off. It’s optional, and free: turn it on for any rental.

Can I use it on a computer?

Yes. Sign in at business.nohm.app to see your rentals on a big screen.
