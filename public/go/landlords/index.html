Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. Listings, unlimited applications, tenant invites, repair requests and messages are free, forever. You pay $7 per rental, per month only once you turn on rent collection for that rental.

Does NOHM take a cut of my rent?

No. Tenants pay by bank, straight to your account. Each bank payment carries a $7 processing fee, and you choose whether you or your tenant covers it.

Why does it ask for a card?

A card on file is needed to add a rental, to keep NOHM safe from fraud. You’re not charged on the Free plan.

Can I bring the tenants I already have?

Yes. Invite them from the app. They accept on their phone, or on the web at nohm.app, and you’re connected.

Who fixes things?

Your tenant sends the repair with photos, and you approve it with a tap. It goes to a local NOHM pro, and you both see it happen. NOHM is available wherever local pros have joined, and grows as more sign on.

What does Autopilot do?

It answers your tenants and handles repairs within the limit you set, so you’re basically 90% hands-off. It’s optional: $19.99 per rental, per month.

Can I use it on a computer?

Yes. Sign in at business.nohm.app to see your rentals on a big screen.
