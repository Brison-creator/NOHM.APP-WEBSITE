Check a NOHM document.

Every paper NOHM prints carries a Document ID at the foot of each page. Type it to see what NOHM issued under it, and when.

Check the file too

On the next page you can choose the PDF you were sent. Your browser compares it with the file NOHM issued and says whether it is certified, exactly as issued. The file never leaves your device.

What it shows

The kind of paper, its title and the date NOHM issued it. Never amounts, names or anything private.
