The full NOHM Standard

One standard. Every job. Every pro.

The companies are independent. The standard is not. Every contractor on NOHM agrees to the same basic rules, so you know what to expect before anyone knocks.

  • Show up when they say they will.
  • Communicate clearly.
  • Explain the price before the work begins.
  • Get your approval before doing any additional work.
  • Treat your home, and the people in it, with respect.
  • Do the work professionally.
  • Document what was done.
  • Keep payments inside NOHM.
  • If something goes wrong, address it.

Different companies. One NOHM Standard.

Fair both ways

The standard applies to homeowners too.

Good pros deserve good customers. When you book through NOHM, you agree to:

  • Give accurate job details and the right service address.
  • Be available, and give the pro access to the property.
  • Communicate respectfully.
  • Approve the work before it begins.
  • Pay through NOHM.
  • Report safety, payment or no-show problems accurately.

If something goes wrong

We step in when it matters.

If a pro doesn’t show or abandons the job, behaves unsafely or inappropriately, or charges you for something you didn’t approve, tell us. Contact NOHM Support and we’ll take it from there.

It’s always something. NOHM it.
