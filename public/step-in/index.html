Most things are between you and your pro. A few things are ours.

Who handles what.

Like a rideshare app: you and your pro sort out the job. NOHM steps in on safety, no-shows and payments.

NOHM steps in

  • No-show or abandoned jobThe pro didn’t come, or left and didn’t come back.
  • Unsafe or inappropriate conductAnything that made you feel unsafe in your home.
  • Charges you didn’t approveA payment problem inside NOHM.

You and your pro

  • Quality of the work
  • Finishing or fixing the job
  • Scheduling and follow-ups
  • Pricing questions before you approve

You can always report it.

  1. Report it in the appAnything that went wrong, even if it’s between you and your pro.
  2. NOHM reviews itReports count toward a pro’s standing, the same way ratings work for rideshare drivers.
  3. Standing mattersPros who don’t meet the NOHM Standard can lose their place on NOHM.
