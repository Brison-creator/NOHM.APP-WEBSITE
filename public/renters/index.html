Questions.

Does NOHM cost me anything?

No. NOHM is free for renters. Your landlord pays for the repairs they approve.

How do I join?

Your landlord invites you. You’ll get a text with a code. Download NOHM, enter the code and you’re connected to your home.

My landlord isn’t on NOHM. Can I still use it?

Tell them about it. Landlords add their properties and invite tenants in a few minutes.

What can I send a request for?

Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, appliances, roofing and general repairs. Add photos, a description and whether it’s urgent.

What happens after I send it?

Your landlord gets it right away. When they approve, a local pro is sent and you can follow along. If they decline, you’ll see why.

Can I pay rent in NOHM?

Yes. See your rent and due date on your home screen and pay it in the app.

How much is a rental application?

The application fee is $45, paid when you submit it.

What if it’s an emergency?

Mark it urgent and your landlord can send a pro the same day. If there’s fire, flooding, a gas smell or anyone is in danger, call 911 first.
