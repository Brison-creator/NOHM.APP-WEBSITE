Indiana notice of order to remove property left behind

Tells the tenant the court ordered their property removed, and where it will be stored: personally served before you move it.

At a glance.

  • WhenThe court awarded you possession and ordered the tenant’s property removed.
  • The lawInd. Code § 32-31-4-2; Ind. Code § 32-31-4-3
  • StageMove-out

What to know.

  • Property a reasonable person would conclude the tenant left when they vacated and surrendered possession is abandoned, and you aren’t liable for it (Ind. Code § 32-31-4-2(a)–(b)); a lease can’t define abandonment differently.
  • Otherwise, ask the court that awarded you possession for an order allowing removal of the tenant’s property (§ 32-31-4-2(d)). You may deliver it to a warehouse or a court-approved storage facility only after this notice, of the order and of the facility’s identity and location, is personally served on the tenant at their last known address (§ 32-31-4-3(a)). Attach a copy of the order.
  • Exempt property (§ 32-31-4-1: among it medical items, tools of the trade, a week’s clothing, blankets and children’s school items) must be released without payment (§ 32-31-4-3(b)).
  • The storage facility may sell property not claimed within 45 days after the tenant receives this notice (§ 32-31-4-5, as amended in 2025).
  • Never take or keep a tenant’s property to make them pay (Ind. Code § 32-31-5-5(a)).
  • Read from an unofficial copy of the Indiana Code current to 2024 (the official site couldn’t be opened), checked against the 2025 and 2026 session laws.
