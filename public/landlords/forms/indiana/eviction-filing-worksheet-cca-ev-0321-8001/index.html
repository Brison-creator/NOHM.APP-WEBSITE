Indiana eviction filing worksheet (CCA-EV-0321-8001)

Indiana’s statewide small claims eviction form: this names it, fills in what to write on it from the lease, and sets out the steps and deadlines.

The facts.

  • WhenThe notice ran out and the tenant hasn’t left.
  • The lawInd. Code § 33-29-2-4(b); Ind. Small Claims Rules 2, 3, 4, 16; Ind. Code § 32-30-3-10
  • StageCourt papers

What to know.

  • The Small Claims Complaint and Clerk’s Notice of Claim for Possession, Rent Due and Damages is approved statewide by the Indiana Supreme Court’s Coalition for Court Access; some counties (Marion, for one) use their own. Ask the clerk which to file.
  • In small claims a company may be represented by counsel, its owner, or a designated full-time employee with the certificate and affidavit Small Claims Rule 8(C) requires. A building manager can’t sue in their own name: the landlord must bring it.
  • Waste, a crime by a tenant or guest affecting others’ safety, or lies on the application: an emergency possessory order is a separate, faster petition (Ind. Code § 32-31-6). Unpaid rent isn’t a ground for it.
  • Bring the lease, the notice and how you served it, and the rent ledger.
  • Read from an unofficial copy of the Indiana Code current to 2024 (the official site couldn’t be opened), checked against the 2025 and 2026 session laws.
