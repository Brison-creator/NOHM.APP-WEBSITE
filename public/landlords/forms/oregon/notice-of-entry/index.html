Oregon notice of entry

Tells the tenant when you’ll come in, and why.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenAt least 24 hours before you enter.
  • The lawOr. Rev. Stat. § 90.322(1)(f)
  • StageDuring the lease

What to know.

  • Give at least 24 hours’ actual notice and enter only at reasonable times, unless you and the tenant agree otherwise for a specific entry (Or. Rev. Stat. § 90.322(1)(f)). If the tenant says no after getting it, you may not enter.
  • In an emergency you may enter without notice; if the tenant wasn’t there, give actual notice within 24 hours after, with the date and time, the emergency and who entered (§ 90.322(1)(b)).
  • A tenant’s written repair request lets you enter for that repair for 7 days (§ 90.322(1)(c)).
  • An unlawful entry can cost at least one month’s rent (§ 90.322(8)).
