The facts.

  • WhenThe notice ran out and the tenant hasn’t left.
  • The lawOr. Rev. Stat. §§ 105.110–105.168; Or. Rev. Stat. § 90.395
  • StageCourt papers

What to know.

  • Oregon’s courts use statewide eviction forms. Clerks expect them, so NOHM doesn’t write the complaint: copy these values onto the OJD forms (or use the court’s iForms at courts.oregon.gov/iforms).
  • Attach a copy of the notice you relied on to the complaint (Or. Rev. Stat. § 105.124(3)).
  • An agent or employee can appear for you, without a lawyer (§ 105.130(4)).
