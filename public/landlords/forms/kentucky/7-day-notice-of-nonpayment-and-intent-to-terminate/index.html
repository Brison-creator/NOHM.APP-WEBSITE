Kentucky 7-day notice of nonpayment and intent to terminate

Tells the tenant the rent is unpaid and the lease ends if it isn’t paid within 7 days of getting the notice.

At a glance.

  • WhenRent is past due (Kentucky has no grace period).
  • The lawKRS 383.660(2); KRS 383.560(3)(c); KRS 446.030(1)(a)
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • These rules apply where the city or county adopted the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (KRS 383.500). There is no official list; advocates list Louisville/Jefferson County, Lexington/Fayette County, Oldham and Pulaski Counties, and cities including Covington, Newport, Florence, Bellevue, Dayton, Georgetown, Shelbyville and Barbourville. Ask your city or county.
  • Outside those areas no statute sets a notice to pay rent: the lease decides. The court’s complaint form (AOC-216) still asks the date you gave written notice to vacate, so serve this notice anyway; NOHM never gives less than 7 days.
  • Rent is due without demand when the lease says; Kentucky has no grace period (KRS 383.565(2)). The 7th day moves to the next business day if it falls on a weekend or legal holiday (KRS 446.030(1)(a)).
  • Taking rent while knowing of the default waives your right to end the lease for it, unless you both agree otherwise after the default (KRS 383.675).
  • Hand it to the tenant, or send it by registered or certified mail (KRS 383.560(3)(c)); regular mail and posting aren’t ways the Act allows. NOHM adds 3 days if you mail it.
