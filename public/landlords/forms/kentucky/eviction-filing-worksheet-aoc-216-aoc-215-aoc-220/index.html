What to know.

  • Kentucky’s District Courts use the AOC’s forcible detainer forms. File AOC-216 filled in from this worksheet; this worksheet isn’t filed.
  • AOC-216 is sworn: sign it in front of the clerk or a notary. It certifies you are the owner or the attorney.
  • A business (an LLC or a corporation) must have an attorney file the complaint: Kentucky’s Court of Appeals has held one filed by a non-lawyer, a property manager included, is void. NOHM read only summaries of those cases.
  • Under the Act a notice by mail counts when it is mailed by registered or certified mail (KRS 383.560(3)(c)); NOHM counts from the date you enter.
