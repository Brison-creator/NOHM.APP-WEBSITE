Kentucky demand for possession

A written demand to hand the home back, the step before filing to evict.

At a glance.

  • WhenThe lease ended, or a notice ended it, and the tenant is still there.
  • The lawKRS 383.200(3); KRS 383.695(4)
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • Kentucky sets no period for this notice, but the court’s complaint form (AOC-216) asks the date you gave the tenant written notice to vacate: give one before you file. NOHM fills in 7 days.
  • NOHM’s rent, breach and month-to-month notices are written notices to vacate too. Use this one when a lease for a fixed term ran out, or a tenancy ended another way.
  • Outside the Act’s areas, after a lease of less than a year ends you may recover possession without demand or notice if you file within 30 days after it ends; if you don’t, you can’t file until 60 days after it ended. For a lease of a year or more it is 90 days, then one year (KRS 383.160). The notice is good practice either way.
  • Hand it to the tenant or send it by registered or certified mail, and keep proof.
