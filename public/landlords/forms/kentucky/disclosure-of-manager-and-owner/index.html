Kentucky disclosure of manager and owner

Tells the tenant in writing who manages the home and who receives legal papers and notices for the owner.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenAt or before the start of the tenancy, and again whenever it changes.
  • The lawKRS 383.585
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Required in writing at or before the start of the tenancy, and kept current (KRS 383.585(1)–(2)). This is a rule of the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act, which applies only where the city or county adopted it (KRS 383.500): Louisville/Jefferson County, Lexington/Fayette County, Oldham and Pulaski Counties, and cities including Covington, Newport, Florence, Bellevue, Dayton, Georgetown, Shelbyville and Barbourville, by advocates’ lists (there is no official one). Ask your city or county.
  • Anyone who fails to give it becomes your agent for service of process and for receiving notices and demands (KRS 383.585(3)).
