Kentucky move-in damage listing

The list of damage already in the home, with what each would cost to repair, signed by you and the tenant before the deposit is paid.

At a glance.

  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Required where the Act applies: give the tenant this listing of existing damage, with the estimated cost of repairing each, before they pay the deposit; let them inspect; both sign (KRS 383.580(2)). Without it, and the move-out listing, you may not keep any of the deposit (§ 383.580(4)). This is a rule of the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act, which applies only where the city or county adopted it (KRS 383.500): Louisville/Jefferson County, Lexington/Fayette County, Oldham and Pulaski Counties, and cities including Covington, Newport, Florence, Bellevue, Dayton, Georgetown, Shelbyville and Barbourville, by advocates’ lists (there is no official one). Ask your city or county.
  • Outside the Act’s areas it isn’t required; it is still good practice.
  • Also required under the Act, in writing at or before the start of the tenancy: who manages the home and who receives legal papers for the owner (KRS 383.585), and where the separate deposit account is, with its number (§ 383.580(1)). Use NOHM’s two disclosures.
  • Statewide: a lease can’t penalize a tenant for calling the police or emergency help (KRS 383.302). Under the Act a lease can’t make the tenant waive their rights, confess judgment, pay your attorney’s fees, or excuse you from liability (KRS 383.570).
  • A home built before 1978 needs the federal lead-based paint disclosure and pamphlet.
