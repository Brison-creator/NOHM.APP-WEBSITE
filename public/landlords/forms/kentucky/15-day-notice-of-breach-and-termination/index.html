Kentucky 15-day notice of breach and termination

Says what the tenant did wrong; the lease ends on the date in it, at least 15 days out, unless they fix it first.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenThe tenant materially broke the lease (other than rent).
  • The lawKRS 383.660(1); KRS 383.560(3)(c); KRS 446.030(1)(a)
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • These rules apply where the city or county adopted the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (KRS 383.500). There is no official list; advocates list Louisville/Jefferson County, Lexington/Fayette County, Oldham and Pulaski Counties, and cities including Covington, Newport, Florence, Bellevue, Dayton, Georgetown, Shelbyville and Barbourville. Ask your city or county.
  • The statute sets the termination date at least 14 days after receipt, and also says the agreement ends if the breach isn’t remedied in 15 days (KRS 383.660(1)). NOHM uses 15 days, which meets both, and lets the tenant remedy it up to that date.
  • Describe exactly what the tenant did or didn’t do: the notice must specify the acts and omissions that are the breach.
  • If substantially the same act or omission recurs within 6 months, the Act lets you end the agreement on at least 14 days’ written notice specifying the breach and the date, with no chance to remedy it (KRS 383.660(1)). NOHM’s notice always offers the remedy; giving more than the law requires is allowed.
  • A tenant who refuses you lawful access can be given this notice too (KRS 383.700(1)).
  • A notice to vacate substantially based on acts that violated a tenant’s protective order, or led to one, is a defense to eviction (KRS 383.300(3)(b)).
  • Outside the Act’s areas no statute sets this notice: the lease decides. The complaint form (AOC-216) has an “Other violation of lease” box and asks the date of your written notice to vacate.
  • Hand it to the tenant, or send it by registered or certified mail (KRS 383.560(3)(c)); regular mail and posting aren’t ways the Act allows. NOHM adds 3 days if you mail it.
