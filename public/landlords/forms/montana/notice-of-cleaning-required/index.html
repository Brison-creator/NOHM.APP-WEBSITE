Montana notice of cleaning required

Tells the tenant what cleaning is left undone; they have 24 hours to do it before you charge the deposit for it.

At a glance.

  • WhenBefore you deduct cleaning from the deposit.
  • The lawMont. Code Ann. § 70-25-201(3)
  • StageMove-out

What to know.

  • You can’t deduct cleaning from the deposit until you’ve given this notice; the tenant then has 24 hours to do the cleaning (MCA 70-25-201(3)).
  • Give it as any notice (in hand, or mail with a certificate of mailing or certified mail: served 3 days after mailing). If the tenant left without notice, you may leave it in a conspicuous place in the unit and tell them by email, phone or text (MCA 70-25-201(3)(c)).
  • Not needed where the rental agreement was already terminated and your claim is pending in court.
  • Montana’s courts publish an optional “Letter Additional Cleaning” for the same purpose (courts.mt.gov/Forms/landlord).
