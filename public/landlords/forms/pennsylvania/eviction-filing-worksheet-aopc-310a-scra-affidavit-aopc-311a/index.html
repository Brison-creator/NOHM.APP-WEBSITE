What to know.

  • The complaint must be on the state’s form (Pa.R.Civ.P.M.D.J. 503A), and courts refuse altered ones, so NOHM doesn’t write it: copy these values onto AOPC 310A.
  • For each tenant who is a person, attach the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act affidavit (Rule 503D).
  • An LLC or corporation may appear through an officer, or an employee or agent with personal knowledge and an officer’s written authorization filed for this case (Rule 207).
  • Philadelphia evictions go to Municipal Court, with their own rules; this worksheet is for the magisterial district courts.
