Pennsylvania notice of deposit escrow account

Tells the tenant the name and address of the bank holding their deposit, and the amount.

At a glance.

  • WhenWhen you deposit a security deposit over $100 in escrow.
  • The law68 P.S. § 250.511b(a)
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Deposits over $100 go in an escrow account at a bank regulated by the Federal Reserve, the Comptroller of the Currency or the Pennsylvania Department of Banking, and you must then tell each tenant in writing the bank’s name and address and the amount (68 P.S. § 250.511b(a)).
  • From the second anniversary of the deposit it must earn interest; you may keep 1% a year as an administrative fee and pay the rest of the interest to the tenant each year on the lease’s anniversary (§ 250.511b(b)–(c)). A bond can take the escrow’s place (§ 250.511c).
  • Cap: two months’ rent in the first year of the lease, one month’s from the second year on, and after five years a rent increase can’t raise the deposit (§ 250.511a).
