Minnesota eviction action complaint

The eviction filing, with the facts and attachments Minnesota requires, and your verification.

Updated November 7, 2026

The facts.

  • WhenThe 14-day notice or the notice to quit has run, the lease has ended, or the tenant breached it, and they haven’t left.
  • The lawMinn. Stat. § 504B.285, subd. 1; Minn. Stat. § 504B.321, subds. 1, 1a, 3
  • StageCourt papers
  • KindCourt paper

What to know.

  • File it in the district court of the county where the rental is (Housing Court in Hennepin and Ramsey). The court issues the summons, with the statements on legal help and financial help that the law requires.
  • Attach the lease and its addenda; for nonpayment, the 14-day notice (without it the court must dismiss the case and expunge it); and any notice to quit (Minn. Stat. § 504B.321, subds. 1(d), 3). The court must dismiss and expunge a case that doesn’t comply (subd. 5).
  • Never name a tenant’s minor child as a defendant (§ 504B.213).
  • The appearance is 7 to 14 days after the summons issues. Serve the summons and complaint at least 7 days before it by personal service, or substitute service at the home; mail and post only after two tries on different days, one between 6 and 10 p.m.; file the affidavits of service at least 3 days before. If you usually email or text the tenant, also send them a dated electronic notice of the hearing at least 7 days before (§ 504B.332).
  • If you win, the writ of recovery issues, usually stayed up to 7 days; the tenant has 15 days to appeal; the officer gives 24 hours to leave (§§ 504B.345, 504B.365, 504B.371).
  • In a nonpayment case the tenant can pay what’s owed, with interest, costs and a $5 attorney’s fee, until possession is delivered, unless you also claimed a material breach (§ 504B.291).
  • A management agent or other non-lawyer may bring the case for you, but not try it to a jury or appeal it (§ 481.02, subd. 3). Hennepin and Ramsey ask for a Power of Authority (HOU103), and a company may need a lawyer to sign there (not confirmed by NOHM).
  • The court’s own form, HOU102 Eviction Action Complaint, covers the same ground; NOHM couldn’t open the court’s site to compare it. Sign the verification in front of a notary.
