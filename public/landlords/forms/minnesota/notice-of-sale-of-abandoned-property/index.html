Minnesota notice of sale of abandoned property

Lists what the tenant left, and how long you’ll hold it before you dispose of it.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenThe tenant abandoned the home and left things behind: at least 14 days before you sell them.
  • The lawMinn. Stat. § 504B.271, subd. 1
  • StageMove-out

What to know.

  • Give it at least 14 days before the sale: in person, or by first class AND certified mail (the 14 days start when you mail it); and post it on the premises at least two weeks before (Minn. Stat. § 504B.271).
  • The sale can be no sooner than 28 days after you learn of the abandonment, or it reasonably appears the tenant abandoned the home, whichever is later.
  • If the tenant asks in writing for their property, return it within 24 hours (48 hours, not counting weekends and holidays, if you stored it elsewhere). Withholding it can cost up to twice their actual damages or $1,000.
  • After an eviction, don’t use this: the officer’s procedure applies (§ 504B.365, subd. 3).
