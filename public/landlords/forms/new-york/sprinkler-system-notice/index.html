New York sprinkler system notice

The bold-face notice every New York residential lease must give of whether the premises have a working sprinkler system.

At a glance.

  • WhenWith every residential lease: attach it when the lease is signed or renewed.
  • The lawN.Y. Real Prop. Law § 231-a
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Every residential lease must give conspicuous notice, in bold face type, of whether the leased premises have a maintained and operative sprinkler system, and if they do, the last date it was maintained and inspected (Real Prop. Law § 231-a).
