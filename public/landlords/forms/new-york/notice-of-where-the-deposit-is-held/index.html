New York notice of where the deposit is held

Tells the tenant, in writing, the bank where you deposited their security and how much.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenWhen you deposit the security in a bank.
  • The lawN.Y. Gen. Oblig. Law § 7-103(2)
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • The deposit stays the tenant’s money, held in trust, never mixed with yours (Gen. Oblig. Law § 7-103(1)).
  • When you put it in a bank, tell each tenant who paid it, in writing, the bank’s name and address and the amount (§ 7-103(2)). The bank must have a place of business in New York.
  • A building with 6 or more units: the deposit must be in an interest-bearing account at the prevailing rate (§ 7-103(2-a)). In an interest-bearing account, you may keep one percent a year on the deposit for administration.
