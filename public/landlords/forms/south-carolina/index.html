Notices before court.

5-day notice of nonpayment of rent

Tells the tenant the rent is unpaid and that the rental agreement ends if it isn’t paid in 5 days.

When
Rent is unpaid when due (once per lease term; not needed if your lease has the statute’s clause).
The law
S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-710(B); S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-240(B)(3), (D); SCRCP 6(a)
Good to know
  • You may end the agreement once rent is unpaid 5 days after it was due (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-710(B)). NOHM gives the tenant 5 days after this notice, the safer reading of the statute.
  • You need give this notice only once per lease term, and not at all if your written lease has the statute’s “IF YOU DO NOT PAY YOUR RENT ON TIME” clause in conspicuous language; that clause covers a month-to-month tenancy after the term too (§ 27-40-710(B)). NOHM’s move-in forms include it.
  • Five days, not counting weekends and holidays; a last day on one moves on (SCRCP 6(a)).
  • Hand it to the tenant, or mail it registered or certified to the address they gave for notices (or their last known residence); proof of mailing is notice (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-240(B)(3)). Court rules add 5 days for some papers served by mail (SCRCP 6(e)); it isn’t settled that this applies here, so NOHM adds them.
  • The same 5-day notice applies when a tenant conducts or permits illegal activity in the unit or uses it other than as a home (§§ 27-40-540, 27-40-710(B)).
  • Attorney’s fees for a nonpayment not in good faith are available only if a lawyer represents you (§ 27-40-710(C)).
  • South Carolina needs no separate demand for possession: after the tenancy ends you may apply for ejectment (S.C. Code Ann. §§ 27-37-10(A), 27-40-770(c)). So NOHM has no separate demand form for South Carolina.

14-day notice to remedy a breach

Says what the tenant did wrong: remedy it in 14 days, or the rental agreement ends.

When
The tenant broke the lease, or a duty affecting health and safety (not rent).
The law
S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-710(A); S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-240(B)(3), (D); SCRCP 6(a)
Good to know
  • Describe exactly what the tenant did or failed to do: the statute requires the notice to specify it (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-710(A)).
  • A breach of the tenant’s health-and-safety duties (§ 27-40-510) you can fix by repair, replacement or cleaning: after 14 days’ written notice you may do the work and bill the tenant (§ 27-40-720).
  • Illegal activity, or using the unit other than as a home, uses the 5-day notice instead (§§ 27-40-540, 27-40-710(B)).
  • Hand it to the tenant, or mail it registered or certified to the address they gave for notices (or their last known residence); proof of mailing is notice (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-240(B)(3)). Court rules add 5 days for some papers served by mail (SCRCP 6(e)); it isn’t settled that this applies here, so NOHM adds them.
  • South Carolina has no shorter notice for a repeated breach.
  • Attorney’s fees for a wilful breach are available only if a lawyer represents you (§ 27-40-710(C)).
  • South Carolina needs no separate demand for possession: after the tenancy ends you may apply for ejectment (S.C. Code Ann. §§ 27-37-10(A), 27-40-770(c)). So NOHM has no separate demand form for South Carolina.

30-day notice to end a month-to-month

Ends a month-to-month tenancy on a date at least 30 days out.

When
You want a month-to-month tenant out; no reason is needed.
The law
S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-770(b); S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-240(B)(3)
Good to know
  • Any day at least 30 days out works: South Carolina doesn’t require it to end a rental period, and rent is apportioned day to day unless the lease says otherwise (S.C. Code Ann. §§ 27-40-770(b), 27-40-310(c)).
  • A week-to-week tenancy needs 7 days’ written notice instead (§ 27-40-770(a)).
  • Hand it to the tenant, or mail it registered or certified to the address they gave for notices (or their last known residence); proof of mailing is notice (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-240(B)(3)). Court rules add 5 days for some papers served by mail (SCRCP 6(e)); it isn’t settled that this applies here, so NOHM adds them.
  • No just-cause rule, but a notice found to be retaliation bars recovering possession for 75 days (§ 27-40-910). A victim of documented domestic abuse may end the lease early (§ 27-40-350).
  • A holdover not in good faith can owe your attorney’s fees; a wilful one up to three months’ rent or twice actual damages, whichever is greater (§ 27-40-770(c)).
  • South Carolina needs no separate demand for possession: after the tenancy ends you may apply for ejectment (S.C. Code Ann. §§ 27-37-10(A), 27-40-770(c)). So NOHM has no separate demand form for South Carolina.

Court papers.

Eviction filing worksheet (SCCA 732, 733A, 734)

What to copy onto the magistrate’s Application for Ejectment, where to get it and the forms that follow, and each step and deadline.

When
The notice ran out, or the rent is 5 days late and your lease has the statute’s clause, and the tenant hasn’t left.
The law
S.C. Code Ann. §§ 27-37-10 to 27-37-160; S.C. Code Ann. §§ 27-40-710, 27-40-790, 27-40-800
Good to know
  • South Carolina’s magistrates use the Judicial Branch’s statewide forms, so NOHM doesn’t write the application: copy these values onto SCCA 732 and swear to it before a notary.
  • A business can be represented by a non-lawyer officer, agent or employee with written authorization from its president, owner or chief executive (Magistrate Court Rule 21). A licensed broker-in-charge or property manager may complete the form for you (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-710(C)).
  • Attach the lease or other written proof that the tenant rents from you.

Move-in.

Lease addendum: notice for late rent

The statute’s “IF YOU DO NOT PAY YOUR RENT ON TIME” clause, signed with the lease, so no separate 5-day notice is needed.

When
At signing, or added to a running lease.
The law
S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-710(B); S.C. Code Ann. § 27-37-10(B)
Good to know
  • With this clause in the written lease, in conspicuous language, you needn’t send a separate 5-day notice before applying for ejectment for nonpayment, during the term or a month-to-month after it (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-710(B)).
  • It also makes nonpayment within 5 days of the due date legal notice that you may begin ejectment (§ 27-37-10(B)). The paper prints it in bold, boxed.
  • Have every tenant sign it, and keep it with the lease.

Move-in condition checklist

The written record of the home’s condition when the tenant moves in, signed by both of you.

When
Give it at move-in, and keep the signed copy.
Good to know
  • South Carolina doesn’t require a move-in checklist. It is good practice: walk through with the tenant, both sign it, and keep a copy for the deposit.
  • In writing at or before move-in: the name and address of an owner, or of the owner’s agent for service of process and notices (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-420(a)). Keep it current.
  • Put the “IF YOU DO NOT PAY YOUR RENT ON TIME” clause in your lease (NOHM’s lease addendum) to skip the 5-day notice (§ 27-40-710(B)).
  • More than 4 adjoining units with different deposit standards for different tenants: post the standards or give each prospective tenant a statement of them (§ 27-40-410(c)).
  • To enter for regularly scheduled services without notice, put that right conspicuously in the lease (§ 27-40-530(b)(2)).
  • Built before 1978: give the federal lead-based paint disclosure and pamphlet.

During the lease.

Notice of entry

Tells the tenant when you’ll come in, and why.

When
At least 24 hours before you enter.
The law
S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-530(c)
Good to know
  • Give at least 24 hours’ notice and enter only at reasonable times (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-530(c)).
  • Without notice you may enter in an emergency (a coming storm can count); between 9 a.m. and 6 p.m. for regularly scheduled services your lease conspicuously provides for, announcing yourself first; or between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. for services the tenant asked for, announcing yourself first (§ 27-40-530(b)).
  • Don’t use entry to harass the tenant.

Notice of rent change

Changes a month-to-month tenant’s rent from a rent due date.

When
At least 30 days before the new rent starts.
Good to know
  • No South Carolina statute sets notice for a rent increase. NOHM asks for 30 days, the notice that ends a month-to-month tenancy (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-770(b)), and starts the new rent on a due date.
  • During a fixed-term lease you can change the rent only if the lease allows it.
  • South Carolina has no rent cap, and counties and cities can’t regulate rents (§ 27-39-60). An increase in retaliation, above fair market rent, is barred (§ 27-40-910(a)).

Move-out.

Security deposit: itemized deductions

The written list of what you kept from the deposit, and why, with the refund.

When
Within 30 days after the tenancy ends and you get the home back.
The law
S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-410(a)
Good to know
  • Itemize deductions in writing and send the amount due within 30 days after the tenancy ends and the tenant gives back possession, or after the tenant demands it, whichever is later (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-410(a)).
  • If the tenant gave no forwarding address in writing and you didn’t know where they were, mailing it to their last known address protects you from damages.
  • Wrongful withholding: three times the amount wrongly kept, plus attorney’s fees (§ 27-40-410(b)).
  • South Carolina doesn’t cap deposits or require interest or a separate account.

Notice of property left behind

Lists what the tenant left, and how long you’ll hold it before you dispose of it.

When
After the tenancy ended and the tenant left things behind.
The law
S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-730(d)–(e)
Good to know
  • Only for property worth $500 or less in all, after the unit was abandoned or the lease ended and the tenant took most of their things or shut off their utilities (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-730(d)). Abandoned means 15 days’ unexplained absence after rent went unpaid, or at once if they ended their utilities (§ 27-40-730(a)–(b)).
  • South Carolina requires no notice for it. NOHM fills in 7 days to hold the property, its own choice, to head off disputes.
  • Worth more than $500: you may remove it only through ejectment (§ 27-40-730(e); SCCA 737, Affidavit of Abandonment).
  • After an ejectment, property put on the street is removed after 48 hours, not counting weekends and holidays (§ 27-40-710(D)).
