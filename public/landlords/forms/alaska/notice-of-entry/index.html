Alaska notice of entry

Tells the tenant when you’ll come in, and why.

At a glance.

  • WhenAt least 24 hours before you enter.
  • The lawAlaska Stat. § 34.03.140(c)
  • StageDuring the lease

What to know.

  • Give at least 24 hours’ notice, enter only at reasonable times, and with the tenant’s consent (AS 34.03.140(c)).
  • You may enter to inspect, make repairs or improvements, supply services, remove your own property not covered by the lease, or show the home to buyers, lenders, tenants, workers or contractors (AS 34.03.140(a)). In an emergency you may enter without consent (§ 34.03.140(b)); also during an absence of more than 7 days (§ 34.03.230(b)).
  • Abusing access, or refusing it, has remedies under AS 34.03.300.
