Alaska disclosure of manager and owner

Tells the tenant in writing who manages the home and who receives notices and legal papers for the owner.

Updated November 7, 2026

The facts.

  • WhenAt or before the start of the tenancy, and again whenever it changes.
  • The lawAlaska Stat. § 34.03.080
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Required at or before the start of the tenancy, and kept current (AS 34.03.080(a)–(b)).
