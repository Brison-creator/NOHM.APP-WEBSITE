New Hampshire move-in condition checklist

The written record of the home’s condition when the tenant moves in, signed by both of you.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenGive it at move-in; the tenant has 5 days to return it.
  • The lawN.H. Rev. Stat. Ann. § 540-A:6, I(b)–(c)
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • When you take a deposit, you must tell the tenant in writing that conditions needing repair or correction should be listed and given to you within 5 days of occupancy (RSA 540-A:6, I(b)–(c)). This form does it.
  • Exempt: an owner renting a single-family home who owns no other rental property, or units in an owner-occupied building of 5 units or fewer, unless the tenant is 60 or older (RSA 540-A:5, I).
  • Also give the signed deposit receipt (the next form) unless the tenant paid by personal, bank or agency check.
  • Before taking an application fee, disclose its amount in writing and any background or credit check you require (RSA 540-A:3, VIII). Let the tenant pay rent at least one way other than electronically (RSA 540-A:3, X).
  • Owners of restricted property file with the town or city clerk the name, address and phone of an in-state agent for service of process, within 30 days of becoming the owner (RSA 540:1-b).
  • A home built before 1978 also needs the federal lead-based paint disclosure.
