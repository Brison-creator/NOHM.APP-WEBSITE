Delaware eviction filing worksheet (J.P. Civil Form No. 1, CF83A)

What to put on the Justice of the Peace Court’s complaint for summary possession, where to get the forms, and the deadlines after you file.

Updated November 7, 2026

The facts.

  • WhenThe notice ran out and the tenant hasn’t paid, remedied the breach, or left.
  • The law25 Del. C. §§ 5701–5707; 25 Del. C. § 5702A; 25 Del. C. §§ 5715–5717
  • StageCourt papers
  • KindCourt paper

What to know.

  • File in the Justice of the Peace Court that hears civil cases in the county where the rental is (25 Del. C. § 5701), on J.P. Civil Form No. 1, checking “Summary Possession (LLT) – Residential Unit”.
  • Attach a copy of the notice you served: the complaint must have it as an exhibit (§ 5707(4)).
  • A corporation, LLC or other entity filing without a lawyer must file Form 50, Certificate of Representation (Supreme Court Rule 57).
  • Every case goes to mediation first (eviction diversion). File the Landlord’s Affidavit of Participation in Eviction Diversion Program (CF83A) at least 5 days before the hearing, with a copy to the tenant.
  • In a rent case the tenant can redeem the tenancy by paying everything the court sets, with costs and fees, at any time before the writ is executed, unless they had 2 nonpayment judgments in the past 18 months (§ 5716A). The court gives its effective date as September 1, 2026; NOHM couldn’t confirm that date from the session law.
  • The court also names a breakdown-of-amounts form (CF13LT) and a right-of-redemption motion (CF20RR); NOHM hasn’t read them.
