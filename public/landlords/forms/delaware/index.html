Notices before court.

7-day notice to pay rent

Demands the past-due rent within 7 days, or the landlord may file to end the rental agreement.

When
Rent is past due and the 5-day late-fee window has passed (8 days if you keep no payment office in the county).
The law
25 Del. C. § 5502(a); 25 Del. C. § 5502(c); 25 Del. C. § 5112; 25 Del. C. § 5113; 25 Del. C. § 5606
Good to know
  • Wait until the late-fee grace period has passed: 5 days after the due date, or 8 if you keep no office in the county where rent can be paid (25 Del. C. §§ 5501(d), 5502(a)).
  • The 7 days count from the day after it’s given or sent; mailing adds nothing. If the 7th day is a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, the period runs to the next business day (25 Del. C. § 5112).
  • You may not refuse rent paid by or for the tenant; you may limit payment to the methods the lease allows (25 Del. C. § 5502(c)). If all rent due is paid after you file, you may not go on with the case (§ 5502(d)).
  • Attach the Courts’ Right to Representation notice (25 Del. C. § 5606): courts.delaware.gov/forms/download.aspx?id=224598.
  • Email and text messages are not valid ways to give notice in Delaware. Keep the return receipt or the certificate of mailing (PS Form 3817): either is evidence of service (25 Del. C. § 5113(b)).

7-day notice to remedy a breach

Names the rule or lease term broken and gives the tenant 7 days to remedy it.

When
The tenant broke a material rule or covenant of the lease (not late rent).
The law
25 Del. C. § 5513(a); 25 Del. C. § 5112; 25 Del. C. § 5113; 25 Del. C. § 5606
Good to know
  • Name the rule or lease term the tenant broke, and what they did: the notice must substantially specify it (25 Del. C. § 5513(a)(1)).
  • A breach you can fix yourself (cleaning, a repair, a replacement): you may do it and bill the tenant the actual reasonable cost, due as additional rent on receipt (§ 5513(a)(2)).
  • A breach that is also a material breach of a code, ordinance or statute lets you terminate and file (§ 5513(a)(3)). One that causes or threatens irreparable harm to a person or property lets you terminate at once on notice and file, without the 7 days (§ 5513(b)).
  • Late rent isn’t a breach under this section: use the 7-day notice to pay rent.
  • A landlord renting 3 or fewer rooms in their own home has its own rules (25 Del. C. § 5512).
  • Attach the Courts’ Right to Representation notice (25 Del. C. § 5606): courts.delaware.gov/forms/download.aspx?id=224598.
  • Email and text messages are not valid ways to give notice in Delaware. Keep the return receipt or the certificate of mailing (PS Form 3817): either is evidence of service (25 Del. C. § 5113(b)).

60-day notice to end a month-to-month

Ends a month-to-month 60 days after the 1st of the month following the day the tenant gets it.

When
You want a month-to-month tenant out, or not to renew a lease (60 days before it ends).
The law
25 Del. C. § 5106(d); 25 Del. C. § 5112; 25 Del. C. § 5113
Good to know
  • Sixty days, counted from the 1st of the month after the tenant actually gets it (25 Del. C. § 5106(d)). NOHM adds 3 days for a notice you mail.
  • A lease with a fixed term also needs 60 days’ written notice before it ends, and the notice must say the agreement terminates on its expiration date (§ 5106(c)). Without it, a lease of a year or more becomes month-to-month (§ 5108(a)).
  • Delaware has no separate demand for possession: once this notice runs out, a tenant who stays is holding over, and you can file for summary possession (25 Del. C. § 5702(1)). Holdover damages can reach double the monthly rent, prorated by the day (§ 5515(b)).
  • Delaware sets no separate notice for a week-to-week tenancy; a tenancy with no stated term is month-to-month (§ 5106(b)).
  • The Right to Representation notice is required with this notice when the tenancy can be ended only for good cause (§ 5602(b)(1)); the Courts’ notice says to attach it to every termination notice, so NOHM lists it.
  • Attach the Courts’ Right to Representation notice (25 Del. C. § 5606): courts.delaware.gov/forms/download.aspx?id=224598.
  • Email and text messages are not valid ways to give notice in Delaware. Keep the return receipt or the certificate of mailing (PS Form 3817): either is evidence of service (25 Del. C. § 5113(b)).

Court papers.

Eviction filing worksheet (J.P. Civil Form No. 1, CF83A) Court

What to put on the Justice of the Peace Court’s complaint for summary possession, where to get the forms, and the deadlines after you file.

When
The notice ran out and the tenant hasn’t paid, remedied the breach, or left.
The law
25 Del. C. §§ 5701–5707; 25 Del. C. § 5702A; 25 Del. C. §§ 5715–5717
Good to know
  • File in the Justice of the Peace Court that hears civil cases in the county where the rental is (25 Del. C. § 5701), on J.P. Civil Form No. 1, checking “Summary Possession (LLT) – Residential Unit”.
  • Attach a copy of the notice you served: the complaint must have it as an exhibit (§ 5707(4)).
  • A corporation, LLC or other entity filing without a lawyer must file Form 50, Certificate of Representation (Supreme Court Rule 57).
  • Every case goes to mediation first (eviction diversion). File the Landlord’s Affidavit of Participation in Eviction Diversion Program (CF83A) at least 5 days before the hearing, with a copy to the tenant.
  • In a rent case the tenant can redeem the tenancy by paying everything the court sets, with costs and fees, at any time before the writ is executed, unless they had 2 nonpayment judgments in the past 18 months (§ 5716A). The court gives its effective date as September 1, 2026; NOHM couldn’t confirm that date from the session law.
  • The court also names a breakdown-of-amounts form (CF13LT) and a right-of-redemption motion (CF20RR); NOHM hasn’t read them.

Move-in.

Move-in condition checklist

The written record of the home’s condition when the tenant moves in, signed by both of you.

When
Give it at move-in, and keep the signed copy.
Good to know
  • Delaware doesn’t require a move-in checklist. It’s good practice: it is your evidence of damage beyond normal wear and tear when the deposit comes back.
  • Give the tenant the Attorney General’s Summary of the Delaware Residential Landlord-Tenant Code at the start of the tenancy; without it, the tenant may plead ignorance of the law (25 Del. C. § 5118). The current edition is on the Attorney General’s site.
  • Give the Right to Representation notice at lease signing, and at the first renewal (25 Del. C. § 5606).
  • Every written lease must prominently disclose the names and usual business addresses of the owners or their resident agents (§ 5105(a)); give the tenant a free copy of the signed lease (§ 5105(b)).
  • Hold the deposit in a security deposit account at a federally insured bank with an office in Delaware, and tell the tenant where it is (§ 5514(b)). The deposit can’t be more than one month’s rent for a lease of a year or more, unless the unit is furnished (§ 5514(a)).
  • Before renting, inspect for bed bugs and tell the tenant if a unit next door is infested or being treated (§ 5317). A tenant-employee gets the written disclosure § 5123(b) describes. Give a receipt for rent paid in cash (§ 5501(e)).
  • Coming: the Rights and Responsibilities Guide (§ 5124, by June 10, 2027 at the latest) and Delaware’s lead-safe certificate disclosures (§§ 5406–5407, by March 1, 2028). Today, the federal lead-paint disclosure applies to homes built before 1978.

During the lease.

Notice of entry

Tells the tenant when you’ll come in, and why.

When
At least 2 days before you enter.
The law
25 Del. C. § 5509(b)
Good to know
  • At least 48 hours’ notice, and enter only between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m.; choose a time window inside those hours (25 Del. C. § 5509(b)).
  • No notice is needed for repairs the tenant asked for, or in an emergency. For showing the unit to prospective tenants or buyers, the tenant may waive the 48 hours in a signed addendum.
  • Don’t abuse the right of entry or use it to harass the tenant.

Notice of rent change

Changes a month-to-month tenant’s rent from a rent due date.

When
At least 60 days before the new rent starts.
The law
25 Del. C. § 5107(a)
Good to know
  • To renew with a new rent, give at least 60 days’ written notice before the rental agreement expires, saying the new rent and the date it takes effect (25 Del. C. § 5107(a)).
  • Applying the 60 days to a month-to-month is the common and the safe reading; the statute speaks of a rental agreement’s expiration and doesn’t name month-to-month tenancies. NOHM couldn’t confirm it.
  • The tenant is deemed to accept unless they give notice of termination at least 45 days before the last day of the term; a tenant who rejects the renewal has given notice to end the tenancy (§ 5107(b)–(c)).
  • No statewide cap. An increase within 90 days after a tenant’s protected complaint is presumed retaliatory (§ 5516(c)).

Move-out.

Security deposit: itemized deductions

The written list of what you kept from the deposit, and why, with the refund.

When
Within 20 days after the tenancy ends and you get the home back.
The law
25 Del. C. § 5514(f)
Good to know
  • Within 20 days after the rental agreement ends, send the itemized list of damages with the estimated cost of repair of each, and pay the difference; if you don’t, you’ve acknowledged no damages are due (25 Del. C. § 5514(e)–(f)).
  • You may keep: damage beyond normal wear and tear, or that painting and ordinary cleaning can’t correct; unpaid rent and late charges, including rent for an early termination or abandonment; and reasonable costs of re-renting after an early termination (§ 5514(c)).
  • Send it to the forwarding address the tenant gave you in writing, or to the rental unit; if they gave none, you aren’t liable for double damages, but they may claim the deposit for a year (§ 5514(h)).
  • Wrongful withholding costs double the amount withheld (§ 5514(g)).

Notice of property left behind

Lists what the tenant left, and how long you’ll hold it before you dispose of it.

When
After the tenancy ended and the tenant left things behind.
The law
25 Del. C. § 5715(e)
Good to know
  • Delaware has no procedure for belongings a tenant leaves without a court case: property is handled after a judgment for summary possession.
  • At the writ, you may remove and store what’s left for 7 days at the tenant’s expense; then it’s abandoned (25 Del. C. § 5715(e)). The writ itself carries the court’s notice to the tenant.
  • After a judgment for a tenant who abandoned the unit, if there’s no appeal you may remove and store the items; seven days after the appeal period ends they’re abandoned (25 Del. C. § 5507(e)).
  • Photograph the property before you move or dispose of it.
