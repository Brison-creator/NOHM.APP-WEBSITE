Washington notice of property left behind

Lists what the tenant left, and how long you’ll hold it before you dispose of it.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenA tenant who owed rent abandoned the home and left property.
  • The lawRCW 59.18.310(2)
  • StageMove-out

What to know.

  • This is for a tenant who defaulted in rent and, by words or actions, showed they won’t resume the tenancy (RCW 59.18.310(1)). Mail it first class to the last known address and any other address you know (RCW 59.18.310(2)).
  • The sale or disposal can be no sooner than 45 days after the notice is mailed or delivered; 7 days if everything left is worth $250 or less, except personal papers, family pictures and keepsakes (RCW 59.18.310(2)). Hold any excess from a sale for a year.
  • After a writ of restitution, RCW 59.18.312 governs instead (storage on the tenant’s request; 30 days’ notice of sale). For a sole tenant who died, RCW 59.18.595.
