Missouri demand for possession

A written demand to hand the home back, the step before filing to evict.

At a glance.

  • WhenThe lease ended, or a notice ended it, and the tenant is still there.
  • The lawMo. Rev. Stat. § 534.030.1; Mo. Rev. Stat. § 534.050
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • A tenant who holds over after the tenancy ends, and after a written demand for possession, is guilty of unlawful detainer (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 534.030.1). Serve this demand after the lease, the 10-day notice or the month-to-month notice has ended the tenancy.
  • Missouri sets no waiting period after the demand; NOHM fills in 3 days. Give more if you like.
  • Serve it by handing it to the person in possession, or leaving it with someone over 15 who lives there or is in charge; if no one is there, post it on the premises (§ 534.050). Anyone but a sheriff or other officer must swear to the return: sign it before a notary.
  • You don’t need this demand to file for rent and possession; the demand for rent is enough there.
