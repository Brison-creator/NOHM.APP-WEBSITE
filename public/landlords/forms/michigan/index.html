Notices before court.

7-day demand for possession (rent not paid)

Demands the rent owed: pay it, or move, within 7 days of service.

When
Rent is due and unpaid (Michigan has no grace period).
The law
Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5714(1)(a); Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5716; Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5718(1); Mich. Comp. Laws § 8.6
Good to know
  • This demand has what Michigan requires (MCL 600.5716): the premises, the reason, the time to act, the amount due, dated and signed by you, your attorney or your agent. The courts’ own form is DC 100a; either may be used.
  • Serve it any time rent is due and unpaid: Michigan sets no grace period. Hand it to the tenant; give it on the premises to a household member of suitable age, asking them to pass it on; mail it first class; or send it electronically if the tenant agreed in writing. Posting it on the door is not a way Michigan allows.
  • Mail counts as served on the next mail-delivery day; NOHM counts the next business day, which can be a day later than the law’s.
  • Keep a copy that shows when and how you served it: it goes with your complaint (DC 102a).
  • Mobile home park tenants need just cause and the courts’ form DC 100d.
  • If the tenant pays the judgment and the costs within the time the judgment gives, no order of eviction issues (MCL 600.5744(7)).

7-day demand for possession (damage or health hazard)

Demands the tenant repair serious damage or remove a health hazard they caused, or move, within 7 days.

When
The tenant caused serious, continuing damage or a health hazard.
The law
Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5714(1)(d); Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5716; Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5718(1); Mich. Comp. Laws § 8.6
Good to know
  • Use this only for serious, continuing damage or a health hazard the tenant caused, and file within 90 days after you discovered it (MCL 600.5714(1)(d)). The courts’ form is DC 100b.
  • Michigan has no general notice to fix other lease violations. For another breach, end the lease under its own termination clause (MCL 600.5714(1)(c)(i)) and serve the courts’ notice to quit, DC 100c.
  • Two other grounds have their own court forms: illegal drugs on the premises, with a lease clause and a police report, 24 hours (DC 100e; MCL 600.5714(1)(b)); a tenant, household member or guest who caused or threatened physical injury, with the police notified, a 7-day notice to quit (DC 100c; MCL 600.5714(1)(e)).
  • If, within 90 days before you file, the tenant tried to enforce their rights or complained to a government agency, the court may presume the case is retaliation (MCL 600.5720).

Notice to quit (1 month, month-to-month)

Ends a month-to-month tenancy on a date at least one month out.

When
You want a month-to-month tenant out, no fault needed.
The law
Mich. Comp. Laws § 554.134(1); Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5718(1); Mich. Comp. Laws § 8.6
Good to know
  • One month’s notice ends a month-to-month tenancy; for weekly rent, a week’s notice is enough (MCL 554.134(1)). The date doesn’t have to be the end of a rental period.
  • A year-to-year tenancy ends one year after the notice is served (MCL 554.134(3)).
  • Michigan has no separate demand for possession: once this notice ends the tenancy, or a lease runs out on its own date, you can file without another demand (MCL 600.5714(1)(c)). The courts’ form for this notice is DC 100c.
  • Public housing and mobile home park tenants need just cause (MCL 600.5714(2), (3)).
  • If, within 90 days before you file, the tenant tried to enforce their rights or complained to a government agency, the court may presume the case is retaliation (MCL 600.5720).

Court papers.

Eviction filing worksheet (DC 102a, DC 102b, DC 102c, DC 104, DC 105, DC 107)

Which of the courts’ landlord-tenant forms to use, what to write on them, and the steps and deadlines after you file.

When
The demand or notice to quit has run, or the lease has expired, and the tenant hasn’t left.
The law
Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5704; Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5714; Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5735; Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5744; MCR 4.201
Good to know
  • Michigan’s district courts use the State Court Administrative Office’s landlord-tenant forms. NOHM doesn’t write a complaint for you; this sheet tells you which form to use and what to put on it.
  • Mailing is a required part of serving the summons in Michigan (MCR 4.201(D)); NOHM didn’t read the whole rule, so follow the court’s instructions for it.
  • A company (LLC or corporation) or a property manager needs a lawyer in district court, as several courts tell landlords (not confirmed in a statute or court rule by NOHM).
  • Either side may demand a jury (form MC 22). The tenant’s answer form is DC 111c.
  • If you mailed the demand, NOHM counts it served on the next business day; mail is delivered on Saturdays, so the law’s date can be a day or two sooner.

Move-in.

Inventory checklist (move-in)

The written record of the home’s condition when the tenant moves in, signed by both of you.

When
Give it at move-in; the tenant has 7 days to return it.
The law
Mich. Comp. Laws § 554.608
Good to know
  • Give the tenant two blank copies at move-in, listing every item you own in the unit; the tenant returns one within 7 days after taking possession (MCL 554.608). Use the same checklist at move-out, as the termination checklist.
  • Other papers Michigan requires: the security deposit notice within 14 days after the tenant takes possession (NOHM has it; MCL 554.603); in the lease, your name and address for notices and the Truth in Renting notice (MCL 554.634); the statement on release from a lease for domestic violence, sexual assault or stalking, in the lease or given at signing (MCL 554.601b); if the lease allows more than one way to pay rent, at least one without a fee (MCL 554.633(1)(o)); and the federal lead disclosure for homes built before 1978.
  • The deposit can be no more than 1½ months’ rent (MCL 554.602).

Security deposit notice (within 14 days)

Tells the tenant where to send you notices, who holds the deposit, and that they must give a forwarding address within 4 days after moving out.

When
Within 14 days after the tenant takes possession.
The law
Mich. Comp. Laws § 554.603
Good to know
  • Give it no later than 14 days after the tenant takes possession: without it you may not require a security deposit (MCL 554.603).
  • Keep the deposit in a regulated financial institution; to use the money, you must first file a cash or surety bond with the Secretary of State (MCL 554.604).
  • The deposit can be no more than 1½ months’ rent (MCL 554.602).

During the lease.

Notice of entry

Tells the tenant when you’ll come in, and why.

When
Before you enter for an inspection, a repair or a showing.
Good to know
  • Michigan has no statute on entry or notice before it; your lease decides. NOHM suggests at least 24 hours.
  • Entering to make needed repairs or inspect is allowed only as the law provides; anything more can be unlawful interference with the tenant’s possession (MCL 600.2918(3)(b)).

Notice of rent change

Changes a month-to-month tenant’s rent from a rent due date.

When
At least 30 days before the new rent starts.
Good to know
  • Michigan sets no notice for a rent increase. NOHM asks for 30 days and starts the new rent on a due date. For a month-to-month, the safest way is to end the tenancy with a notice to quit and offer a new one at the new rent.
  • During a lease, change the rent only as the lease allows.
  • Michigan has no rent cap, and local governments can’t control rents (MCL 123.411).

Move-out.

Security deposit: itemized deductions

The written list of what you kept from the deposit, and why, with the refund.

When
Within 30 days after the tenancy ends and you get the home back.
The law
Mich. Comp. Laws § 554.609
Good to know
  • Mail it within 30 days after the tenancy ends, with a check or money order for any balance. Since September 21, 2026, you may instead send the balance by direct deposit or electronic transfer within 10 days after mailing the notice (MCL 554.609(3)).
  • Miss the 30 days and you agree that no damages are due: return the whole deposit right away (MCL 554.610).
  • If the tenant disputes it, you must sue within 45 days after the tenancy ends to keep the amount in dispute, unless an exception applies; failing that costs double the amount kept (MCL 554.613).
  • If the tenant didn’t give a forwarding address within 4 days after moving out, you don’t have to send this, as long as your deposit notice told them so (MCL 554.603).

Notice of property left behind

Lists what the tenant left, and how long you’ll hold it before you dispose of it.

When
After the tenancy ended and the tenant left things behind.
Good to know
  • Michigan has no statute for this notice. NOHM fills in 30 days to pick things up; it isn’t a statutory period.
  • Use it only once you have possession lawfully. After a court’s order of eviction, the officer decides what is abandoned and removes it (MCL 600.5744(1)–(2)). Without one, you may retake a unit only if you believe in good faith the tenant abandoned it, after diligent inquiry you have reason to believe they don’t intend to return, and current rent is unpaid (MCL 600.2918(3)(c)).
  • Taking or keeping a tenant’s belongings otherwise is unlawful interference: $200 or actual damages for each time, three times that for forcible removal (MCL 600.2918).
