Michigan 7-day demand for possession (rent not paid)

Demands the rent owed: pay it, or move, within 7 days of service.

At a glance.

  • WhenRent is due and unpaid (Michigan has no grace period).
  • The lawMich. Comp. Laws § 600.5714(1)(a); Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5716; Mich. Comp. Laws § 600.5718(1); Mich. Comp. Laws § 8.6
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • This demand has what Michigan requires (MCL 600.5716): the premises, the reason, the time to act, the amount due, dated and signed by you, your attorney or your agent. The courts’ own form is DC 100a; either may be used.
  • Serve it any time rent is due and unpaid: Michigan sets no grace period. Hand it to the tenant; give it on the premises to a household member of suitable age, asking them to pass it on; mail it first class; or send it electronically if the tenant agreed in writing. Posting it on the door is not a way Michigan allows.
  • Mail counts as served on the next mail-delivery day; NOHM counts the next business day, which can be a day later than the law’s.
  • Keep a copy that shows when and how you served it: it goes with your complaint (DC 102a).
  • Mobile home park tenants need just cause and the courts’ form DC 100d.
  • If the tenant pays the judgment and the costs within the time the judgment gives, no order of eviction issues (MCL 600.5744(7)).
