What to know.

  • Michigan’s district courts use the State Court Administrative Office’s landlord-tenant forms. NOHM doesn’t write a complaint for you; this sheet tells you which form to use and what to put on it.
  • Mailing is a required part of serving the summons in Michigan (MCR 4.201(D)); NOHM didn’t read the whole rule, so follow the court’s instructions for it.
  • A company (LLC or corporation) or a property manager needs a lawyer in district court, as several courts tell landlords (not confirmed in a statute or court rule by NOHM).
  • Either side may demand a jury (form MC 22). The tenant’s answer form is DC 111c.
  • If you mailed the demand, NOHM counts it served on the next business day; mail is delivered on Saturdays, so the law’s date can be a day or two sooner.
