  • The lawMich. Comp. Laws § 554.608
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Give the tenant two blank copies at move-in, listing every item you own in the unit; the tenant returns one within 7 days after taking possession (MCL 554.608). Use the same checklist at move-out, as the termination checklist.
  • Other papers Michigan requires: the security deposit notice within 14 days after the tenant takes possession (NOHM has it; MCL 554.603); in the lease, your name and address for notices and the Truth in Renting notice (MCL 554.634); the statement on release from a lease for domestic violence, sexual assault or stalking, in the lease or given at signing (MCL 554.601b); if the lease allows more than one way to pay rent, at least one without a fee (MCL 554.633(1)(o)); and the federal lead disclosure for homes built before 1978.
  • The deposit can be no more than 1½ months’ rent (MCL 554.602).
