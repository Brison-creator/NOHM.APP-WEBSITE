Nebraska praecipe for writ of restitution (CC 3:24)

Asks the clerk to issue the writ that has the sheriff or constable put you back in possession.

At a glance.

  • WhenThe court entered judgment for restitution of the premises.
  • The lawNeb. Rev. Stat. § 76-1446
  • StageCourt papers
  • KindCourt paper

What to know.

  • The writ issues at your request after judgment, and restores possession on a date not more than 10 days after it issues (§ 76-1446).
  • This follows the Nebraska Supreme Court’s form CC 3:24, Praecipe for Writ of Restitution/Execution. If your clerk asks for that form itself, it’s at supremecourt.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/CC-3-24.pdf.
  • Remove property left on the premises only as the Disposition of Personal Property Landlord and Tenant Act allows (§ 76-1446; the property form).
