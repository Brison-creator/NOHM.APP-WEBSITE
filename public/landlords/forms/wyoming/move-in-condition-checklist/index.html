What to know.

  • No Wyoming statute requires a move-in checklist. It’s good practice: it shows the unit’s condition when you deduct for damage beyond reasonable wear and tear or for cleaning (W.S. 1-21-1208(a)).
  • Wyoming requires the rental agreement, and a written notice when the deposit is taken, to say whether any part of the deposit is nonrefundable (W.S. 1-21-1207; NOHM’s notice). Any lack of hot water, heat or other basics must be agreed in writing by both of you (W.S. 1-21-1202(a)). Homes built before 1978 need the federal lead-based paint disclosure.
