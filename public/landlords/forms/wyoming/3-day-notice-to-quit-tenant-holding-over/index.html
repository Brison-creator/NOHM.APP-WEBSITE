Wyoming 3-day notice to quit (tenant holding over)

The written notice to leave Wyoming requires before you file against a tenant who stayed after the lease ended.

At a glance.

  • WhenThe lease ended, or the agreement’s notice ended the tenancy, and the tenant is still there.
  • The lawWyo. Stat. § 1-21-1003; Wyo. Stat. § 1-21-1002(a)(i); Wyo. Stat. § 34-2-128
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • A tenant who stays after the lease ends must get a written notice to leave at least 3 days before you file (W.S. 1-21-1002(a)(i), 1-21-1003). A lease isn’t renewed by the tenant staying or by your taking rent: that makes only a tenancy by sufferance (W.S. 34-2-128).
  • These are the words of the court’s Notice to Quit Premises (FED Form 01) for a holdover; its instructions say not to change them.
  • Serve it by leaving a copy with the tenant, or, if they can’t be found, at their usual place of abode or business. The server must be 18 or older and signs the Affidavit for Service of Notice to Quit (FED Form 02) before a notary. File on the 4th day after service or later.
