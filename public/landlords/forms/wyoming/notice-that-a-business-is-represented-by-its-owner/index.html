Wyoming notice that a business is represented by its owner

Lets an LLC, corporation or partnership that owns the rental appear in the eviction without a lawyer.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenYour business owns the rental and an owner, shareholder, member or partner will handle the case.
  • The lawWyo. Stat. § 1-21-1017(a)
  • StageCourt papers
  • KindCourt paper

What to know.

  • A business entity may represent itself in an eviction without a lawyer only if an owner, shareholder, member or partner represents it, it owns a majority interest in the property, and it gives the court and all parties this written notice, signed by every owner, shareholder, member or partner (W.S. 1-21-1017(a)). A property manager who isn’t an owner can’t.
  • It doesn’t apply if you seek a judgment over the amount in W.S. 1-21-201 ($6,000). If a lawyer later appears for the business, the tenant is entitled to a continuance (W.S. 1-21-1017(a)(iii)).
  • The court has no form for this notice. File it with the complaint and serve a copy on each tenant.
