North Dakota automatic renewal reminder

The written reminder of the lease’s automatic renewal clause, without which the clause can’t be enforced.

At a glance.

  • WhenAt least 30 days before the lease expires, if it renews automatically.
  • The lawN.D. Cent. Code § 47-16-06.1
  • StageDuring the lease

What to know.

  • In a lease of two months or more, an automatic renewal clause is enforceable only if you remind the tenant of it in writing, delivered personally or by first-class mail, at least 30 days before the lease expires (§ 47-16-06.1).
  • Without the reminder, the lease expires and turns into a month-to-month tenancy on its latest terms.
