Vermont flood hazard disclosure (FEMA flood area)

The separate written disclosure Vermont requires before a lease: whether any part of the rental is in a FEMA mapped special flood hazard area.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenAt or before the lease is signed, as its own document.
  • The law9 V.S.A. § 4466(a)
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Required since June 17, 2024 (Act 181): disclose before entering the lease whether any part of the premises is in a FEMA mapped special flood hazard area, in a separate written document given at or before signing, substantially in the form the Department of Housing and Community Development prescribes (9 V.S.A. § 4466(a)).
  • This form follows the Department’s model form (Rev. 9/2024): the map number and date, the answer, your certification that you checked the map yourself, and the signatures. The Department’s form also has its own notice to tenants about flood insurance; NOHM’s follows it in substance. You may use the Department’s form instead.
  • Look the address up on FEMA’s Flood Map Service Center (msc.fema.gov) before you answer.
