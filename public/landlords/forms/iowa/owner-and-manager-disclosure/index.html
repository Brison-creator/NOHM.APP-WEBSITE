Iowa owner and manager disclosure

The written disclosure Iowa requires before the tenancy starts: who manages the home, who accepts notices and legal papers for the owner, and whether it’s a Superfund site.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenAt or before the start of the tenancy, and again when any of it changes.
  • The lawIowa Code § 562A.13(1); Iowa Code § 562A.13(6)
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Required at or before the start of the tenancy, and kept current (Iowa Code § 562A.13(1)–(2)). If you don’t, the person who signs the rental agreement for you becomes your agent for service and for performing the landlord’s obligations (§ 562A.13(3)).
  • Check whether the property is listed in the EPA’s Superfund information system (CERCLIS) before you answer.
