Nevada 7-day notice to pay rent or quit

Tells the tenant to pay the rent or surrender the home within 7 judicial days, and how to contest it in court.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenRent is past due (a late fee only 3 days after the due date).
  • The lawNev. Rev. Stat. § 40.253(1)–(3); Nev. Rev. Stat. § 40.2512(1)(b); Nev. Rev. Stat. § 40.280(1)
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • In the notice’s details, enter the justice court for the township where the rental is, with its address (for example, Las Vegas Justice Court and its address). The law requires the notice to identify it (NRS 40.253(3)(a)); left blank, the notice prints a line to write it on.
  • Clark County’s justice courts don’t count Fridays as judicial days (their forms say so). NOHM counts Monday to Friday, so in Clark County count the judicial days Monday to Thursday yourself and give that later date.
  • Reno Justice Court expects its own current notice form and may dismiss a case that uses another (Administrative Order 2025-01). For a Washoe County rental, serve the court’s form (washoecounty.gov/rjc/forms).
  • A sheriff, constable, licensed process server or an attorney’s agent must serve it; you can’t serve it yourself (NRS 40.280(1)). The server signs a statement of the date and manner of service with their badge or license number; an attorney’s agent’s needs the attorney’s declaration (NRS 40.280(5)).
  • You may serve it any time after rent is due. If the tenant then pays the rent, you can’t refuse it because collection fees, attorney’s fees or other costs are unpaid (NRS 40.253(11)).
  • Rent paid by the week or less, in a tenancy of 45 days or less, may use a 4-day notice instead (NRS 40.253(1)(b)); NOHM doesn’t draw it.
  • Keep the notice private: it may not be made available for public inspection (NRS 40.2545(5)). Workers affected by a government shutdown have protections (NRS 40.253(12)).
  • If the tenant doesn’t pay, leave, or file an answer by the deadline, file your court’s complaint for summary eviction (the eviction filing worksheet).
