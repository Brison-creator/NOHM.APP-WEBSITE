Massachusetts security deposit receipt

The signed receipt Massachusetts requires when you take a security deposit.

What to know.

  • Give it when you take the deposit, signed by whoever received it (M.G.L. c. 186, § 15B(2)(b)).
  • Put the deposit in a separate, interest-bearing account in a Massachusetts bank, and give the bank receipt within 30 days (§ 15B(3)(a)). Give the statement of condition too (§ 15B(2)(c)).
  • The deposit can be no more than the first month’s rent (§ 15B(1)(b)).
