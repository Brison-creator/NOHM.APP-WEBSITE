Massachusetts statement of condition (move-in)

The signed statement of the home’s condition, with the notice the law requires, that goes with a security deposit.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenGive it at move-in; the tenant has 15 days to return it.
  • The lawM.G.L. c. 186, § 15B(2)(c)
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Required when you take a security deposit: give it when you receive the deposit or within 10 days after the tenancy starts, whichever is later, signed by you or your agent (M.G.L. c. 186, § 15B(2)(c)).
  • List all damage existing in the premises, including any code violation certified by a board of health or building official or found by a court.
  • If the tenant returns a separate signed list of damage, within 15 days give them a copy with your signed agreement, or a clear statement of disagreement, attached.
  • You can’t deduct from the deposit for damage on this statement or the tenant’s list (§ 15B(4)). Keep a record of damage and repairs for each unit, open to the tenant, until 2 years after the tenancy ends (§ 15B(2)(d)).
  • Other papers Massachusetts requires: a signed deposit receipt when you take the deposit, and a bank receipt within 30 days (NOHM has both); a receipt for last month’s rent, saying the tenant is owed 5% interest on it (or the lower rate the bank paid) and should give you a forwarding address (§ 15B(2)(a)); a statement of interest each year (§ 15B(3)(b)); a copy of the signed lease within 30 days (§ 15D); the lead law notification, the latest lead inspection report or letter of compliance, and the tenant certification, before the tenancy starts (c. 111, § 197A; NOHM didn’t check the Department of Public Health’s form titles); the federal lead disclosure for homes built before 1978; your name and contact posted, and the Department of Public Health’s notice of occupants’ rights (105 CMR 410.400); and your fire insurance details within 15 days if the tenant asks (§ 21).
  • Up front you may take only the first month’s rent, the last month’s rent, a deposit of no more than one month’s rent, and the cost of a new lock and key (§ 15B(1)(b)).
