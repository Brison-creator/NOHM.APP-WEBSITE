Virginia 14-day notice of nonpayment (pay or quit)

Tells the tenant the rent is unpaid and that the lease ends if it isn’t paid within 14 days.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenRent is unpaid when due (Virginia sets no grace period; with no written lease, rent is late after the 5th).
  • The lawVa. Code § 55.1-1245(F); Va. Code § 55.1-1200; Va. Code § 55.1-1202; Va. Code § 55.1-1250(A)
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • The notice is 14 days since July 1, 2026 (it was 5). Serve it any time after rent is unpaid when due; the statute sets no grace period. With no written lease, rent is due on the 1st and late after the 5th (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(C)(4)).
  • Serve it by hand, by regular mail to the tenant’s last known residence, electronically if the lease provides for it, or by the sheriff, and keep the certificate of service: the Act’s definition of notice requires one. NOHM adds 3 days when it’s mailed; the Act adds none.
  • The notice includes the acceptance-with-reservation language of Va. Code § 55.1-1250(A), so you can take a partial payment and still evict. If you file, give the court a copy of this notice to serve with the summons.
  • The tenant can still redeem by paying everything owed at or before the first court date, and after judgment up to 48 hours before the eviction (Va. Code § 55.1-1250). A landlord of 4 or fewer units can limit that to once per lease period by telling the tenant in writing.
  • For a tenant with a Housing Choice Voucher or other tenant-based rental assistance, the notice works only if the statewide legal aid number and website are on its first page, in type as large as the rest (Va. Code § 55.1-1202(D)). NOHM prints them in the statements; check they fall on page 1.
  • If a check bounced or a funds transfer was rejected, the notice must demand payment by cash, cashier’s check, certified check or a completed electronic funds transfer (Va. Code § 55.1-1245(F)). Say so in the notice.
  • To claim attorney’s fees or civil recovery later, the notice of Va. Code § 55.1-1202 may be included in this notice.
  • The 14th day can fall on a weekend or holiday; Virginia’s time rule (Va. Code § 1-210) doesn’t clearly move it. Give a later date if you want to be safe.
  • From July 1, 2027, the notice must include a statement of charges and payments over the tenancy or the past 12 months (Va. Code § 55.1-1202(E)), and a landlord of more than 4 units must offer a payment plan when the rent owed is one month’s rent plus late charges or less (Va. Code § 55.1-1245(G)). This notice doesn’t have them yet: don’t use it on or after that date without them.
  • The court won’t order possession unless a copy of a proper termination notice is in evidence (Va. Code § 8.01-126(D)). Keep this one and its certificate of service.
