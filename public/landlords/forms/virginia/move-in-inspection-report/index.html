Virginia move-in inspection report

The written record of the home’s condition when the tenant moves in, signed by both of you.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenWithin 5 days after the tenant moves in; they have 5 days after they get it to object in writing.
  • The lawVa. Code §§ 55.1-1214, 55.1-1215
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Give the tenant this report within 5 days after they move in (Va. Code § 55.1-1214). It is deemed correct unless they object in writing within 5 days after receiving it. Your written policy may let the tenant prepare it, or both of you together, and both sign it.
  • Fill in the mold row: the report must disclose whether there is visible evidence of mold. If there is, the tenant may end the lease, or you remediate it within 5 business days and give a new report (Va. Code § 55.1-1215).
  • Also at the start of a tenancy: offer a written lease and give the Statement of Tenant Rights and Responsibilities with it, both of you signing the Department of Housing and Community Development’s acknowledgment form, and give a signed copy of both within 10 business days (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(B), (H)).
  • The lease must have the fee disclosure statement on its first page (Va. Code § 55.1-1204.1), and you must give the names and addresses of the manager and of an owner or someone authorized to act for the owner (Va. Code § 55.1-1216(A)).
  • Give the renter’s insurance notice before signing if the lease doesn’t require renter’s insurance (NOHM’s form, Va. Code § 55.1-1206(D)), and a certificate that all smoke alarms are present, inspected and working (Va. Code § 55.1-1220(A)(8)).
  • Where they apply: the military air installation (Va. Code § 55.1-1217), defective drywall (§ 55.1-1218) and methamphetamine (§ 55.1-1219) disclosures, and the federal lead-based paint disclosure for homes built before 1978.
