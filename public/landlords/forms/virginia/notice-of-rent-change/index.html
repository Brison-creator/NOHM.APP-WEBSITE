Virginia notice of rent change

Changes a month-to-month tenant’s rent from a rent due date.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenAt least 30 days before the new rent starts.
  • The lawVa. Code § 55.1-1253(D)
  • StageDuring the lease

What to know.

  • For a month-to-month tenancy (or a tenant holding over), a new rent takes effect only on the next rent due date coming 30 days after written notice (Va. Code § 55.1-1253(D)).
  • During a lease, rent changes only if both of you agree in writing (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(I)).
  • A landlord of more than 4 units must give written notice of a rent increase for a renewal term at least 60 days before the lease ends (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(K)); from July 1, 2027, 90 days, with a deadline at least 30 days out for the tenant to say whether they’ll renew.
  • Virginia has no rent cap, and localities can’t adopt their own landlord-tenant rules (Va. Code § 55.1-1201(E)). A retaliatory increase is barred (Va. Code § 55.1-1258).
