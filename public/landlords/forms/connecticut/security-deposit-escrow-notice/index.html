Connecticut security deposit escrow notice

Tells the tenant how much deposit you hold and the bank holding it in escrow.

At a glance.

  • WhenWithin 30 days after you receive a security deposit.
  • The lawConn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-21(h)(4)(A)
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • Put the whole deposit in an escrow account at a financial institution in Connecticut right away (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-21(h)(1)), and send this within 30 days of receiving it.
  • The deposit earns interest at no less than the deposit index the Banking Commissioner sets each year, paid on each anniversary of the tenancy or credited toward rent (§ 47a-21(i)).
