Connecticut notice of rent change

Changes a month-to-month tenant’s rent from a rent due date.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenAt least 31 days before the new rent starts.
  • The lawConn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-4e
  • StageDuring the lease

What to know.

  • A rent increase takes effect only after written notice at least 45 days ahead; for a tenancy of a month or less, notice as long as one full term (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-4e). For a month-to-month NOHM asks for 31 days, the longest month. For a lease longer than a month, give 45.
  • A tenant who doesn’t answer the notice hasn’t agreed to the increase. You can’t raise the rent during a lease’s term unless it allows it.
  • No statewide cap. A tenant protected by § 47a-23c (62 or older, or with a disability, in 5 or more units) may be charged only a fair and equitable increase, which they can challenge before the town’s fair rent commission (§ 47a-23c(c)).
  • No increase within 6 months after a tenant’s good-faith code or fair rent complaint, repair request, or union activity (§ 47a-20).
