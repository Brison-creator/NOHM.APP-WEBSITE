Connecticut security deposit: itemized deductions

The written list of what you kept from the deposit, and why, with the refund.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenWithin 21 days after the tenancy ends and you get the home back.
  • The lawConn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-21(d)(2)
  • StageMove-out

What to know.

  • Send the deposit and accrued interest, less damages, with this statement, within 21 days after the tenancy ends or 15 days after the tenant gives you a forwarding address in writing, whichever is later (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-21(d)(2)).
  • You may deduct unpaid rent or utility charges owed to you, damage beyond the tenant’s duties under § 47a-11, and the reasonable cost of a lock change the tenant didn’t pay; not a condition noted on the move-in walk-through checklist (§§ 47a-21(a), 47a-7c).
  • Include interest at no less than the deposit index for each year, except for any month the tenant was more than 10 days late with rent, unless you charged a late fee for it (§ 47a-21(i)).
  • Missing the deadline costs twice the deposit; missing only the interest, $10 or twice the interest, whichever is more (§ 47a-21(d)(2)).
