Connecticut move-in condition checklist

The written record of the home’s condition when the tenant moves in, signed by both of you.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenGive it at move-in, and keep the signed copy.
  • The lawConn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-7c
  • StageMove-in

What to know.

  • For a lease entered into on or after January 1, 2024, you must offer the tenant a walk-through before they move in. If they ask for one, use the Department of Housing’s Pre-Occupancy Walk-Through Checklist, and both sign duplicate copies; a condition noted on it can’t be charged against the security deposit (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-7c). The checklist is on the Department of Housing’s mandatory forms page.
  • This checklist is a record of its own; it doesn’t replace the Department’s checklist when the tenant asks for the walk-through.
  • From April 1, 2026, every written lease must have the Department of Housing’s Rental Terms Summary Form, completed, as its first page; otherwise a civil penalty of one month’s rent (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-7d(c)).
  • Before or at the start of the tenancy, give the tenant in writing the name and address of the manager and of the person who receives notices and service (§ 47a-6(a)).
  • In a building or complex of 5 or more units, give the Department of Housing’s notice to protected tenants (Form AM-011) at the start of every tenancy and every renewal (§ 47a-23c(e)).
  • Within 30 days of receiving a security deposit, tell the tenant in writing the amount and the escrow bank’s name and address (§ 47a-21(h)(4)(A)): use the escrow notice.
  • Other papers: whether the unit or a unit next to it you own has bed bugs (§ 47a-7a(c)); the fire sprinkler statement in 12-point bold where one is required (§ 47a-3f); a receipt for every cash payment, and the ledger on request (§ 47a-3a); the federal lead-paint disclosure for a home built before 1978.
  • A security deposit can’t be more than two months’ rent, or one month’s for a tenant 62 or older (§ 47a-21(b)).
