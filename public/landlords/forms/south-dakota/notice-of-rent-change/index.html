South Dakota notice of rent change

Changes a month-to-month tenant’s rent from a rent due date.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenAt least 30 days before the new rent starts.
  • The lawS.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-13
  • StageDuring the lease

What to know.

  • A month-to-month’s rent or terms change on written notice given at least 30 days before the end of the month, taking effect when the month ends (S.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-13). Start the new rent on a rent due date.
  • The tenant may end the tenancy on the first of the next month by telling you within 15 days of getting the notice.
  • During a fixed-term lease, change the rent only if the lease allows it.
  • South Dakota has no rent cap. A rent increase above fair market value after a tenant’s complaint or repair request can be retaliation (S.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-27).
