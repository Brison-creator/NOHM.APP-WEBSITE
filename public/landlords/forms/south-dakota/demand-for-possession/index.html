South Dakota demand for possession

A written demand to hand the home back, the step before filing to evict.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenThe lease ended, or a notice ended it, and the tenant is still there.
  • The lawS.D. Codified Laws § 21-3-8; S.D. Codified Laws § 21-16-1(4)
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • Not required before you file: since 2024 you may sue once a tenant holds over after the lease ends (S.D. Codified Laws § 21-16-1(4)). Give it anyway if you want double damages: they need notice to quit and a demand of possession (§ 21-3-8), and the complaint must claim them (§ 21-16-10).
  • South Dakota sets no period for it. NOHM fills in 3 days; give more if you like.
  • Don’t accept rent for time after the lease ended: accepting it renews the tenancy on the same terms, for up to a year (S.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-14).
