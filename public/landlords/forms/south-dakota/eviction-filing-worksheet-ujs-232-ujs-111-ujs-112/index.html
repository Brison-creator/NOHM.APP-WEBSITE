What to know.

  • South Dakota’s courts use the Unified Judicial System’s eviction forms. File those, filled in from this worksheet: a clerk can turn away a homemade complaint.
  • Get them one by one, or all in the landlord’s “Print All” packet, from the UJS self-help page.
  • A corporation or LLC must file through an attorney (the UJS forms’ instructions; S.D. Codified Laws § 15-39-47).
  • Bring the lease, any notice you gave and how, and the rent ledger to the hearing.
