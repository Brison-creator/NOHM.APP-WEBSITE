South Dakota 1-month notice to end a month-to-month

Ends a month-to-month tenancy at the end of a rental month, on at least a month’s notice.

At a glance.

  • WhenYou want a month-to-month tenant out, no fault needed.
  • The lawS.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-15; S.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-4
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • The notice must be at least as long as the rental period, up to one month, and end at the end of a period (S.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-15). A week-to-week tenancy needs one week’s notice, ending at the end of a week.
  • If the tenant stays after the date and you accept rent, the tenancy renews on the same terms (S.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-14). Don’t take rent for any time after the date.
  • A fixed-term lease ends on its own date. Once the tenant holds over you may file; no notice or demand is required (S.D. Codified Laws § 21-16-1(4)). A written notice to quit and demand for possession lets you claim double damages for willful holding over (§ 21-3-8): NOHM’s demand for possession is that paper.
  • A mobile home lot that is being put to another use needs 90 days’ notice to vacate (S.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-31).
  • South Dakota’s law sets no way to serve it. Put it in writing, hand it to the tenant or mail it, and keep proof; NOHM adds 3 days when it’s mailed.
