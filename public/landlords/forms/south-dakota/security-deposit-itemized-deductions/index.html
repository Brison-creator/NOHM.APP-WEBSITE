South Dakota security deposit: itemized deductions

The written list of what you kept from the deposit, and why, with the refund.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenWithin 21 days after the tenancy ends and the tenant gives you a mailing address or delivery instructions.
  • The lawS.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-24
  • StageMove-out

What to know.

  • Return the deposit, or send this statement of the specific reasons for keeping any of it, within 21 days after the tenancy ends and you receive the tenant’s mailing address or delivery instructions (S.D. Codified Laws § 43-32-24, as amended effective July 1, 2026; it was two weeks).
  • Keep only what is reasonably needed for unpaid rent and other money due under an agreement, and to restore the home to its condition at move-in, ordinary wear and tear excepted.
  • If the tenant asks, give an itemized accounting of what you withheld within 45 days after the tenancy ends.
  • Missing these steps forfeits your right to keep any of the deposit; bad faith can add punitive damages of up to $200.
  • No interest or separate account is required. The deposit cap is one month’s rent (§ 43-32-6.1).
