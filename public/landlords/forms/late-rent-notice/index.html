Late rent notices, state by state.

The written notice for unpaid rent, in each state: what it’s called, when you can serve it, and the law behind it. All 50 states and DC.

Page updated November 7, 2026

General information, not legal advice. NOHM isn’t a law firm. Check your state’s current law, or ask a local attorney, before you serve or file anything. Terms

Alabama7-business-day notice to pay rent or the lease endsRent is unpaid when due (Alabama has no grace period).
Alaska7-day notice to quit for unpaid rentRent is unpaid when due (Alaska has no grace period).
Arizona5-day notice to pay rentRent is unpaid when due (Arizona has no grace period).
Arkansas3-day notice to quit (rent not paid)Rent is more than 5 days late.
California3-day notice to pay rent or quitRent is past due (California has no grace period by statute).
Colorado10-day demand to pay rent or moveRent is past due (no grace period for the demand; late fees wait 7 days).
ConnecticutNotice to quit for nonpayment (3 full days, JD-HM-7)Rent is still unpaid 9 days after it was due (from the 10th day; 5 days later if your online payment system blocked it).
Delaware7-day notice to pay rentRent is past due and the 5-day late-fee window has passed (8 days if you keep no payment office in the county).
District of Columbia30-day notice of past due rent (intent to file)At least $600 in rent is past due.
Florida3-day notice to pay rent or deliver possessionRent is past due (Florida sets no grace period; your lease may).
Georgia3-business-day notice to vacate or payRent is past due, on a lease made or renewed on or after July 1, 2024.
Hawaii10-calendar-day notice for failure to pay rentRent is past due (Hawaii has no grace period).
Idaho3-day notice to pay rent or vacateRent is past due (Idaho has no grace period), within a year of the due date.
Illinois5-day notice to pay rentRent is past due (Illinois has no grace period).
Indiana10-day notice to pay rent or vacateRent is past due (Indiana has no grace period).
Iowa3-day notice of nonpayment of rentRent is unpaid when due (Iowa has no grace period).
Kansas10-day notice of nonpayment and notice to quitRent is past due (Kansas has no grace period).
Kentucky7-day notice of nonpayment and intent to terminateRent is past due (Kentucky has no grace period).
Louisiana5-day notice to vacate (rent not paid)Rent is past due and you have ended the lease (Louisiana has no grace period).
Maine7-day notice to quit (rent 7 days or more in arrears)Rent is 7 days or more in arrears.
Maryland10-day notice of intent to file (DC-CV-115)Rent is past due (Maryland has no grace period).
Massachusetts14-day notice to quit for nonpayment of rentRent is due and unpaid (Massachusetts has no grace period).
Michigan7-day demand for possession (rent not paid)Rent is due and unpaid (Michigan has no grace period).
Minnesota14-day notice of possible eviction (rent not paid)Rent or another charge under the lease is due and unpaid (no grace period by statute).
Mississippi3-day notice to pay rentRent is due and unpaid (Mississippi has no grace period).
MissouriDemand for rent (no waiting period)Rent is due and unpaid (wait out any forfeiture time your lease sets).
Montana3-day notice to pay rent or quitRent is past due (Montana has no grace period).
Nebraska7-day notice to pay rent or quitRent is past due (Nebraska has no grace period).
Nevada7-day notice to pay rent or quitRent is past due (a late fee only 3 days after the due date).
New HampshireDemand for rent and 7-day eviction noticeRent is due and in arrears.
New JerseyDemand for payment of rent (optional)Rent is past due (5 business days’ grace for some seniors and benefit recipients).
New Mexico3-day notice of nonpayment of rentRent is unpaid when due (New Mexico has no grace period).
New York14-day rent demandRent is unpaid; if it’s 5 days late, send the late-rent notice by certified mail first.
North Carolina10-day demand for rentRent is past due and your lease has no clause ending it for nonpayment.
North Dakota3-day notice of intention to evict (rent unpaid)Rent is still unpaid 3 days after it was due.
Ohio3-day notice to leave (rent unpaid)Rent is past due (Ohio’s statute has no grace period).
Oklahoma5-day demand for rentRent is past due (Oklahoma has no grace period).
Oregon13-day notice of nonpayment of rentRent is unpaid on the 5th day of the rental period (counting the due date).
Pennsylvania10-day notice to quit (rent unpaid)Rent is due and unpaid (Pennsylvania has no grace period).
Rhode IslandFive-day demand notice for nonpayment of rentRent is 15 days in arrears.
South Carolina5-day notice of nonpayment of rentRent is unpaid when due (once per lease term; not needed if your lease has the statute’s clause).
South DakotaDemand for payment of rent (no notice required)Rent is past due; you can file once it is unpaid 3 days after the due date.
Tennessee14-day notice to pay rent, or the lease endsRent is past due (a late fee only after the 5-day grace period).
Texas3-day notice to pay rent or vacateRent is past due (a late fee only once it’s two full days late).
Utah3-business-day notice to pay or vacateRent is past due (any day after it’s due).
Vermont14-day notice of termination for nonpaymentRent is past due (Vermont has no grace period).
Virginia14-day notice of nonpayment (pay or quit)Rent is unpaid when due (Virginia sets no grace period; with no written lease, rent is late after the 5th).
Washington14-day notice to pay rent or vacateRent is past due (any time after it’s due).
West VirginiaDemand for payment of rent (no notice required)Rent is past due, and any grace period or notice in the lease has passed.
Wisconsin5-day notice to pay rent or vacateRent is past due.
Wyoming3-day notice to quit (rent not paid)Rent is unpaid 3 days after it was due.
