Notices before court.

5-day notice to pay rent or vacate

Pay the rent due within 5 days or move out (30 days for a lease of more than a year).

When
Rent is past due.
The law
Wis. Stat. § 704.17; Wis. Stat. § 704.21

The statute doesn’t set this notice’s exact words: check it before you serve one.

Good to know
  • NOHM counts the 5 days without Saturdays, Sundays and holidays, the longer reading, and gives 30 (counted the same way) for a lease of more than one year.
  • The agency guide says a month-to-month tenant can instead get a 14-day notice to vacate with no chance to pay, and a tenant on a lease of a year or less who is late again within 12 months after a cured 5-day notice can get a 14-day notice with no chance to pay. NOHM doesn’t draw those.
  • No fee or penalty may be charged for not paying a late fee (Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.09(8)(c)).
  • The ways of serving are the ones Wis. Stat. § 704.21 is understood to allow; NOHM couldn’t confirm them from the statute, or whether mailing adds days.

5-day notice to remedy or vacate

Fix the breach within 5 days or move out (30 days for a lease of more than a year).

When
The tenant broke a lease term or damaged the property.
The law
Wis. Stat. § 704.17; Wis. Stat. § 704.21

The statute doesn’t set this notice’s exact words: check it before you serve one.

Good to know
  • NOHM counts the 5 days without Saturdays, Sundays and holidays, the longer reading, and gives 30 for a lease of more than one year.
  • The agency guide says a repeat breach within a year allows a 14-day notice with no chance to cure, and criminal or drug-related activity a 5-day notice to vacate with required contents (Wis. Stat. § 704.17(3m)). NOHM doesn’t draw those.
  • A lease can’t let you end a tenancy because the tenant, or someone living with them, was the victim of a crime there (Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.08(9)).
  • The ways of serving are the ones Wis. Stat. § 704.21 is understood to allow; NOHM couldn’t confirm them from the statute, or whether mailing adds days.

28-day notice to end a month-to-month

Ends a month-to-month tenancy at the end of a rental period, at least 28 days out.

When
You want to end a month-to-month tenancy.
The law
Wis. Stat. § 704.19; Wis. Stat. § 704.21

The statute doesn’t set this notice’s exact words: check it before you serve one.

Good to know
  • A week-to-week tenancy is understood to take 7 days (Wis. Stat. § 704.19); NOHM couldn’t confirm it.
  • No separate demand for possession is needed once the notice runs out: file the eviction (Summons and Complaint, SC-500).
  • You can’t end or refuse to renew a tenancy in retaliation for reporting code violations, organizing, or asserting legal rights (Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.09(5)).
  • An automatic renewal clause can be enforced only if the tenant got a separate written reminder 15 to 30 days before it takes effect (Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.09(3)): use NOHM’s automatic renewal notice.
  • The ways of serving are the ones Wis. Stat. § 704.21 is understood to allow; NOHM couldn’t confirm them from the statute, or whether mailing adds days.

Court papers.

Eviction filing worksheet (SC-500, SC-5100V, SC-512)

Everything to copy onto the court’s Summons and Complaint for an eviction, with the steps after it.

When
Your notice ended the tenancy and the tenant hasn’t left.
The law
Wis. Stat. ch. 799; Wis. Stat. § 799.16; Wis. Stat. § 799.41
Good to know
  • Wisconsin evictions use the statewide Summons and Complaint (SC-500), which may not be modified, so NOHM doesn’t write one: copy these values onto it and file it with the clerk of circuit court.
  • NOHM couldn’t read Wis. Stat. ch. 799 from the official site. Not confirmed, so not on the paper: the return date is understood to be 5 to 25 days after the summons issues (§ 799.05(3)(b)); the court may stay a writ up to 30 days for hardship (§ 799.44(3)); a tenant who wrongfully stays may owe double rent, prorated daily (§ 704.27).
  • NOHM couldn’t find whether a company may appear without a lawyer in small claims (believed to be Wis. Stat. § 799.06). Ask the clerk before an employee appears for an LLC.
  • Bring the notice you served, your proof of serving it, the lease and the rent ledger.

Move-in.

Move-in condition checklist

The written record of the home’s condition when the tenant moves in, signed by both of you.

When
Before you accept a security deposit; the tenant has until a date at least 7 days after the tenancy starts.
The law
Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.06(1)
Good to know
  • Before you accept a security deposit, you must tell the tenant in writing that they may, by a date at least 7 days after the tenancy starts, inspect the unit and report preexisting damages or defects, and ask for the list of damages charged to the previous tenant’s deposit (Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.06(1)(a)). This form does that, with a checklist to note them.
  • If asked, give that list within 30 days after the request, or within 7 days after you notify the previous tenant of their deductions, whichever is later (ATCP 134.06(1)(b)).
  • Wis. Stat. § 704.08 is understood to require a check-in sheet with 7 days to complete it; NOHM couldn’t confirm its words.
  • Before signing or taking a deposit: copies of the lease and rules (ATCP 134.03(1)); a written receipt for any deposit (ATCP 134.03(2)); the names and addresses of the landlord, manager and owner or agent (ATCP 134.04(1)); known uncorrected code violations and habitability problems (ATCP 134.04(2)); and utility charges not in the rent, with how they’re split (ATCP 134.04(3)).
  • Extra deposit deductions, entry terms or a lien on property need a separate signed document titled “NONSTANDARD RENTAL PROVISIONS” (ATCP 134.06(3)(b), 134.09(2)(c), 134.09(4)(b)). A lease that lets you end a tenancy for a crime must include the Notice of Domestic Abuse Protections of Wis. Stat. § 704.14, or it is void (ATCP 134.08(10)); NOHM couldn’t confirm its text. Homes built before 1978 need the federal lead-based paint disclosure.

During the lease.

Notice of entry

Tells the tenant when you’ll come in, and why.

When
At least 12 hours before you enter.
The law
Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.09(2)
Good to know
  • Enter only to inspect, make repairs, or show the unit to prospective tenants or purchasers, with at least 12 hours’ advance notice and at reasonable times (Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.09(2)).
  • No notice is needed if the tenant asks you to come in or agrees ahead of time knowing when, in a health or safety emergency, or when the tenant is absent and entry is reasonably needed to protect the premises (ATCP 134.09(2)(b)).
  • Announce yourself before entering, and say who you are if asked (ATCP 134.09(2)(d)). Other entry terms need a signed “NONSTANDARD RENTAL PROVISIONS” document (ATCP 134.09(2)(c)).

Notice of rent change

Changes a month-to-month tenant’s rent from a rent due date.

When
At least 30 days before the new rent starts.
Good to know
  • NOHM found no Wisconsin rule on notice of a rent increase, and no cap. NOHM asks for 30 days and starts the new rent on a due date: that is NOHM’s practice, not the law.
  • For a month-to-month tenant, a change is understood to take the 28-day notice that ends the tenancy (Wis. Stat. § 704.19); NOHM couldn’t confirm it. During a lease, the rent changes only as the lease allows.
  • Cities and counties can’t control rents (Wis. Stat. § 66.1015, not confirmed). A lease clause allowing an increase because the tenant called the police, health or safety services is void (Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.08(1)).

Notice of automatic renewal

The separate written reminder a tenant must get before a lease’s automatic renewal can be enforced.

When
15 to 30 days before the lease renews or extends itself.
The law
Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.09(3); Wis. Stat. § 704.15
Good to know
  • You can enforce an automatic renewal or extension clause only if the tenant got a separate written notice of it at least 15 days, but no more than 30 days, before its stated effective date (Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.09(3)).
  • Wis. Stat. § 704.15 may require more; NOHM couldn’t read it.

Move-out.

Security deposit: itemized deductions

The written list of what you kept from the deposit, and why, with the refund.

When
Within 21 days after the tenancy ends (or the tenant leaves, as the rule sets).
The law
Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.06
Good to know
  • Deliver or mail the deposit, less what you may withhold, with this written accounting within 21 days (Wis. Admin. Code § ATCP 134.06(2), (4)). The 21 days run from: the day the agreement ends, if the tenant leaves then; if they leave or are evicted earlier, the day the agreement ends or a new tenant’s tenancy begins, whichever comes first; if they leave or are removed later, the day you learn they’ve gone.
  • No deductions for normal wear and tear, or for routine painting or carpet cleaning where there’s no unusual damage (ATCP 134.06(3)(c)). Don’t claim anything you can’t back up (ATCP 134.06(4)(b)).
  • You aren’t in violation just because the post office can’t deliver it (ATCP 134.06(5)). No interest or separate account is required.
  • A violation can cost twice the tenant’s loss plus attorney’s fees (Wis. Stat. § 100.20(5); not confirmed from the statute).
