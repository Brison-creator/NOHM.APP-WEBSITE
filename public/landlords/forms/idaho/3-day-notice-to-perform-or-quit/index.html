Idaho 3-day notice to perform or quit

Tells the tenant to do what the lease requires in 3 days, or hand back the home.

At a glance.

  • WhenThe tenant broke a lease term other than paying rent.
  • The lawIdaho Code § 6-303(3); Idaho Code § 6-304; Idaho Code § 73-109
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • Describe the condition or covenant broken and what the tenant must do.
  • A breach that can’t afterward be performed needs no notice to perform (Idaho Code § 6-303(3)). Assigning, subletting or committing waste against the lease ends it, and needs only a 3-day notice to quit (§ 6-303(4)). Drug activity on the premises is its own ground (§ 6-303(5)). Ask a lawyer for those.
  • A lease-violation case isn’t on the expedited track: the tenant has 21 days to answer the complaint (I.R.C.P. 12(a)), and the Idaho courts’ eviction forms don’t cover it.
  • The prevailing party is entitled to attorney fees (Idaho Code § 6-324).
