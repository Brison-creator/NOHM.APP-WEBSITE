Notices before court.

Demand for payment of rent (no notice required)

A written demand for the rent owed. West Virginia doesn’t require one before filing.

When
Rent is past due, and any grace period or notice in the lease has passed.
The law
W. Va. Code § 55-3A-1(a)(3); W. Va. Code § 37-6-19
Good to know
  • West Virginia requires no notice before filing: the court case itself is the demand (W. Va. Code § 37-6-19). Once rent is in arrears you can file the Petition for Summary Relief (W. Va. Code § 55-3A-1(a)(3)).
  • The lease decides: if it sets a notice or grace period, or a time before reentry, give it first and wait it out (W. Va. Code § 37-6-19). Put that date in the notice.
  • NOHM adds 3 days when it’s mailed, for delivery.

Notice of lease violation (optional)

Tells the tenant what breaks the lease, with any time the lease gives to fix it.

When
The tenant broke a lease covenant or damaged the property.
The law
W. Va. Code § 55-3A-1(a)(3); W. Va. Code § 37-6-19
Good to know
  • West Virginia law doesn’t require this notice: you may petition for summary relief when the tenant has breached a warranty or covenant of the lease, or damaged the property (W. Va. Code § 55-3A-1(a)(3)).
  • The lease decides: if it gives the tenant time to cure, or sets a time before reentry, use that date and wait it out (W. Va. Code § 37-6-19).

Notice to end a periodic tenancy (one full rental period)

Ends a month-to-month (or other periodic) tenancy at the end of a full rental period.

When
You want to end a month-to-month, week-to-week or year-to-year tenancy.
The law
W. Va. Code § 37-6-5
Good to know
  • The notice may be served on the tenant or on anyone holding the premises under them (W. Va. Code § 37-6-5).
  • The lease can set a different notice period, or none (W. Va. Code § 37-6-5). A lease whose term ends on a set date needs no notice at all.
  • A week-to-week tenancy takes one full week’s notice; a year-to-year tenancy takes 3 months’ notice before the end of a year.
  • West Virginia has no separate demand for possession: if the tenant stays after the date, file the Petition for Summary Relief.

Court papers.

Eviction filing worksheet (MLTPTWR, MLTSMWO)

Everything to copy onto the court’s Petition for Summary Relief, with the steps and deadlines after it.

When
Rent is in arrears, or the tenant broke a covenant or damaged the property, or a notice ended the tenancy.
The law
W. Va. Code § 55-3A-1; W. Va. Code § 55-3A-2; W. Va. Code § 55-3A-3
Good to know
  • West Virginia’s magistrate courts use the Judiciary’s own forms, so NOHM doesn’t write the petition: copy these values onto MLTPTWR, sign it before a notary, and file it. The clerk has the forms too.
  • No notice is required before filing, but if the lease sets a notice, grace period or time before reentry, it must run out first (W. Va. Code § 37-6-19).
  • A tenant who stays after a fixed term ends, or after a § 37-6-5 notice runs out, can also be sued for unlawful detainer (W. Va. Code § 55-3-1); the summary petition is the usual route.
  • In circuit court a company generally needs a lawyer (a 2014 Attorney General opinion and a 2012 Supreme Court of Appeals case; NOHM read only summaries). In magistrate court it may appear by an agent (W. Va. Code § 50-4-4a).

Move-in.

Move-in condition checklist

The written record of the home’s condition when the tenant moves in, signed by both of you.

When
Give it at move-in, and keep the signed copy.
Good to know
  • No West Virginia statute requires a move-in checklist. It’s good practice: it shows the home’s condition when deposit deductions come up later.
  • West Virginia requires no other move-in disclosures for an ordinary residential lease. If a pet fee or application fee is nonrefundable, agree on that in writing, or it counts as part of the deposit (W. Va. Code § 37-6A-1(14)). The federal lead-based paint disclosure applies to homes built before 1978.

During the lease.

Notice of entry

Tells the tenant when you’ll come in, and why.

When
Before you enter for an inspection, a repair or a showing.
Good to know
  • No West Virginia statute sets notice for a landlord’s entry: follow your lease. NOHM suggests at least 24 hours, at a reasonable time.

Notice of rent change

Changes a month-to-month tenant’s rent from a rent due date.

When
At least 30 days before the new rent starts.
Good to know
  • No West Virginia statute sets notice for a rent increase, or caps rent. NOHM asks for 30 days and starts the new rent on a due date: that is NOHM’s practice, not the law.
  • For a month-to-month tenant, a change takes the notice that ends the tenancy: one full rental period (W. Va. Code § 37-6-5). NOHM’s date is the first due date at least 30 days out, which leaves a full period; pick another date only if a full period still passes.
  • During a lease, the rent changes only as the lease allows.

Move-out.

Security deposit: itemized deductions

The written list of what you kept from the deposit, and why, with the refund.

When
Within 60 days after the tenancy ends, or 45 days after a new tenant moves in, whichever is sooner.
The law
W. Va. Code § 37-6A-2
Good to know
  • Deliver the deposit, less deductions, with a written itemization within 60 days after the tenancy ends, or within 45 days after a new tenant occupies the home, whichever is sooner (W. Va. Code §§ 37-6A-1(7), 37-6A-2(a)). NOHM warns at 60; if a new tenant moved in, your deadline is earlier.
  • If damages exceed the deposit and need a third-party contractor, give written notice within that time; you then have 15 more days to itemize the damages and the cost of repair (W. Va. Code § 37-6A-2(c)).
  • If it comes back undeliverable, hold it 6 months and hand it over at your place of business within 72 hours of a written request (W. Va. Code § 37-6A-2(g)). Keep records of deductions for a year (§ 37-6A-3).
  • No cap, no interest and no separate account are required. Withholding in bad faith costs the deposit plus one and a half times the amount wrongfully withheld (W. Va. Code § 37-6A-5(a)). A lease can’t waive these rules (§ 37-6A-4).

Posted notice to pay rent within one month (home abandoned)

Posted on a home the tenant left owing rent: pay within a month, or the tenancy ends and you take possession.

When
A tenant who owes rent has abandoned the home.
The law
W. Va. Code § 37-6-6(a)
Good to know
  • Post it in writing in a conspicuous part of the property (W. Va. Code § 37-6-6(a)). If the rent isn’t paid within a month, you are entitled to possession and may enter, and the tenant’s right to the property ends.
  • Then, for anything left behind, use the notice of abandoned property (W. Va. Code § 37-6-6(c)–(d)).

Notice of abandoned property

Lists what the tenant left, and how long you’ll hold it before you dispose of it.

When
The tenant abandoned the home and left property behind.
The law
W. Va. Code § 37-6-6(c)–(d)
Good to know
  • Post it on the property AND mail it first class with a certificate of mailing, in an envelope marked “Please Forward”, to the leased property, any post office box of the tenant you know of, and the latest forwarding address (W. Va. Code § 37-6-6(c)).
  • The date to remove it must be at least 30 days after you mail the notice, or 60 if the tenant told you they’re on active duty in the Armed Forces (W. Va. Code § 37-6-6(d)).
  • Property worth more than $300: if the tenant or a secured party says they’ll remove it and pays reasonable storage and removal costs, store it up to 30 more days (W. Va. Code § 37-6-6(e)).
  • After a court order of possession, W. Va. Code § 55-3A-3(h)–(i) governs property left instead.
