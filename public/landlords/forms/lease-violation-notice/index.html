Lease violation notices, state by state.

The notice for a tenant who broke the lease (other than rent), in each state: what it’s called, when you can serve it, and the law. All 50 states and DC.

Montana14-day notice of noncomplianceThe tenant broke the rental agreement (other than rent).

New JerseyNotice to ceaseThe tenant is disorderly, breaks your rules or a lease covenant, or habitually pays late.
New Mexico7-day notice of noncomplianceThe resident broke the rental agreement (not rent), within the last 30 days or since you learned of it.
New YorkNotice to cure a lease violationThe tenant broke a lease term (10 days to cure where the Good Cause Eviction Law applies).
North CarolinaNotice of lease violationThe tenant broke a lease condition that the lease says ends the tenancy.
North Dakota3-day notice of intention to evict (lease violation)The tenant violated a material term of the written lease.

Oklahoma10-day notice to remedy a breachThe tenant broke the lease or a tenant’s duty under the law (not rent).

PennsylvaniaNotice to quit for a lease breach (15 or 30 days)The tenant broke a condition of the lease.

South Carolina14-day notice to remedy a breachThe tenant broke the lease, or a duty affecting health and safety (not rent).
South DakotaNotice of lease violation and terminationThe tenant broke a lease term (other than rent).
Tennessee30-day notice to remedy a breach, or the lease endsThe tenant broke a lease term (other than rent).
Texas3-day notice to vacate (lease default)The tenant broke a lease term (other than rent).
Utah3-day notice to comply with lease or vacateThe tenant broke a lease term (other than rent).
Vermont30-day notice of termination for breachThe tenant broke a material lease term (other than rent).
Virginia21/30-day notice of material noncomplianceThe tenant materially broke the lease, or a health and safety duty.
Washington10-day notice to comply or vacateThe tenant broke a material lease term or a duty the law sets (not one for money).
West VirginiaNotice of lease violation (optional)The tenant broke a lease covenant or damaged the property.
Wisconsin5-day notice to remedy or vacateThe tenant broke a lease term or damaged the property.
Wyoming3-day notice to quit (renter’s duties broken)The tenant broke a renter’s duty or did a prohibited act (damage, disturbing others, refusing entry).
