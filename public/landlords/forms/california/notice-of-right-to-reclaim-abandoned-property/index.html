California notice of right to reclaim abandoned property

The statute’s own notice to a former tenant: what they left, where to claim it, and the date after which it may be disposed of.

What to know.

  • This is the form Cal. Civ. Code § 1984(a) sets out, word for word.
  • The last day to claim must be at least 15 days after you hand it over, or 18 days after you mail it (Cal. Civ. Code § 1983(b)). Mail it first class to the tenant’s last known address, and if you mail it, send one copy to the vacated premises too; you may also email it if the tenant gave you an email (§ 1983(c)).
  • Property worth $700 or more is sold at a public sale after publication in a newspaper; under $700 you may keep, sell or destroy it (Cal. Civ. Code § 1988). Store it with reasonable care meanwhile.
  • Property that belongs to someone other than the tenant takes a different notice (Cal. Civ. Code § 1985). After a writ of possession, the rules in Code Civ. Proc. § 1174(f)–(i) apply. NOHM doesn’t draw those.
