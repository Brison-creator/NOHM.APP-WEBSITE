Louisiana 5-day notice to vacate (lease broken)

After you dissolve the lease for a failure to perform it, tells the lessee to leave within 5 days of delivery.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenThe lessee broke the lease (other than rent) and you have ended it.
  • The lawLa. Code Civ. Proc. art. 4701; La. Civ. Code art. 2719; La. Code Civ. Proc. art. 4703; La. Code Civ. Proc. art. 5059
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • A lessee must pay the rent, use the home as a prudent administrator for its purpose, and return it as delivered except for normal wear and tear (La. Civ. Code art. 2683). When a party fails to perform, the other may dissolve the lease (art. 2719).
  • A lease can’t be dissolved when the lessee has rendered a substantial part of the performance and the part not rendered doesn’t substantially impair your interest (La. Civ. Code art. 2014). For a smaller breach, a notice to perform within a reasonable time, warning that the lease will otherwise be dissolved, is the safer first step (art. 2015).
  • In a building of 6 or more units (other than an owner-occupied building of 10 or fewer), you may not give a notice to vacate because domestic abuse occurred on the premises and the victim is a lessee or a member of the lessee’s household (La. R.S. 9:3261.1).
  • The 5 days leave out legal holidays because the period is under 7 days (La. Code Civ. Proc. art. 5059(C)). Whether Saturdays are legal holidays (La. R.S. 1:55) wasn’t confirmed, so NOHM skips Saturdays and Sundays too: the longer, safer count.
  • Many Louisiana leases contain the lessee’s written waiver of the notice to vacate. If yours does, you may file the rule to show cause as soon as the lessee’s right of occupancy ends (La. Code Civ. Proc. art. 4701).
  • Hand it to the lessee and keep proof of delivery. You may attach it to a door of the home only if the home is abandoned or closed, or you don’t know where the lessee is (La. Code Civ. Proc. art. 4703). The Code names no mail delivery, so NOHM doesn’t offer it.
