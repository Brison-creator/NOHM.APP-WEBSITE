Louisiana 20-day written demand for rent (oral lease)

Demands the past-due rent in writing; under an oral lease, if it isn’t paid in 20 days the lessee owes your attorney fees for collecting it.

At a glance.

  • WhenRent is past due under an oral lease.
  • The lawLa. R.S. 9:3259
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • For oral leases only (La. R.S. 9:3259(C)). If the lessee doesn’t pay within 20 days after delivery of a written demand that correctly sets out the rent due and owing, they are liable for reasonable attorney fees for collecting it once you win a judgment on it (§ 9:3259(A)).
  • Get the amount right: list only the rent that has become due and delinquent.
  • Deliver it by certified mail to the lessee’s last known address, by hand, or by tacking it on the door of the leased premises (La. R.S. 9:3259(B)).
  • This demand is about money. To get the home back, the lessor dissolves the lease and gives the 5-day notice to vacate.
