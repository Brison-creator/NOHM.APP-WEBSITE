Notices before court.

5-day demand for rent

Demands the past-due rent: if it isn’t paid in 5 days the rental agreement can be terminated. It is also the demand for possession.

When
Rent is past due (Oklahoma has no grace period).
The law
Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 131(B); Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(E); Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 61
Good to know
  • Oklahoma sets no grace period: you may serve this as soon as rent is unpaid when due (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 131(A)–(B)), and before or after you file a suit for the rent.
  • The five days start the day after service. If the fifth day is a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, the period runs to the next day that isn’t (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 61).
  • Serve it on the tenant personally. Only if the tenant can’t be located may you give it to a family member over 12 who lives with them; only if neither can be served may you post it on the unit and mail a copy certified (or by the Post Office’s Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail) (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(E)).
  • When it is posted and mailed, the statute doesn’t say when service is complete; NOHM adds 3 days for the mail.
  • The demand for rent is itself the demand for possession (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 131(B)): if the rent isn’t paid, you can file the eviction affidavit without another notice. That’s why NOHM has no separate demand form for Oklahoma.
  • Oklahoma’s definition of rent (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 102(11)) can reach other lease payments, but NOHM demands rent only. Claim late fees and damages in the eviction affidavit.

10-day notice to remedy a breach

Says what the tenant did wrong: fix it in 10 days, or the rental agreement ends no sooner than 15 days after they get it.

When
The tenant broke the lease or a tenant’s duty under the law (not rent).
The law
Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 132(B); Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(E); Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 61
Good to know
  • Describe exactly what the tenant did or failed to do: the statute requires the notice to specify it.
  • The ten days run from when the tenant receives it. NOHM adds 3 days when it is posted and mailed.
  • Serve it on the tenant personally. Only if the tenant can’t be located may you give it to a family member over 12 who lives with them; only if neither can be served may you post it on the unit and mail a copy certified (or by the Post Office’s Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail) (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(E)).
  • A breach you can fix by a repair, a replacement or cleaning: after 10 days’ written notice you may do the work yourself and bill it as rent on the next due date, but then you can’t end the lease for that breach (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 132(A)).
  • Any later breach after this notice lets you end the lease on written notice, without another chance to fix it (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 132(B)).
  • Imminent, irremediable harm to the premises or a person: you may file the eviction at once (§ 132(C)). Criminal activity that threatens other tenants, or drug-related criminal activity on or near the premises: immediate termination (§ 132(D)). Ask a lawyer before you rely on either.
  • Oklahoma needs no separate demand for possession: a tenant who stays after the tenancy ends or is terminated can be sued for possession right away (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(D)). So NOHM has no separate demand form for Oklahoma.

30-day notice to end a month-to-month

Ends a month-to-month tenancy on a date at least 30 days out.

When
You want a month-to-month tenant out; no reason is needed.
The law
Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(A); Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(E); Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 61
Good to know
  • Any day at least 30 days after service works: Oklahoma doesn’t require it to end a rental period (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(A)).
  • A tenancy of less than a month (week to week) needs 7 days’ written notice instead (§ 111(B)). A lease for a definite term ends on its last day without notice (§ 111(C)).
  • Serve it on the tenant personally. Only if the tenant can’t be located may you give it to a family member over 12 who lives with them; only if neither can be served may you post it on the unit and mail a copy certified (or by the Post Office’s Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail) (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(E)).
  • Oklahoma has no just-cause rule, but you can’t refuse to renew or end a tenancy because a tenant or household member is a victim of domestic violence, sexual violence or stalking (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 113.3).
  • A tenant who stays willfully and not in good faith can owe up to twice the average monthly rent, prorated daily, for each month they stay (§ 111(D)). If you agree to them staying, a new month-to-month tenancy begins.
  • Oklahoma needs no separate demand for possession: a tenant who stays after the tenancy ends or is terminated can be sued for possession right away (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(D)). So NOHM has no separate demand form for Oklahoma.

Court papers.

Eviction affidavit (forcible entry and detainer) Court

The sworn statement that starts the eviction in district court: the premises, the tenants, why, the notice served, and what they owe.

When
The notice ran out, or the lease ended, and the tenant hasn’t left.
The law
Okla. Stat. tit. 12, § 1148.15; Okla. Stat. tit. 12, § 1148.1; Okla. Stat. tit. 41, §§ 111, 131, 132
Good to know
  • File it with the clerk of the district court in the county where the rental is (Okla. Stat. tit. 12, §§ 1148.1, 1148.15). Sign it in front of a notary or the court clerk.
  • Many clerks hand out the Oklahoma Bar Association’s plain-language affidavit (oscn.net, evictions-affidavit.pdf). If yours asks for it, copy these facts onto it.
  • The clerk issues the summons. The hearing is set 5 to 10 days after it issues (§§ 1148.4, 1148.16), and it must be served at least 3 days before: by the sheriff or a process server, by leaving it with someone over 15 living there, or, failing both, by certified mail (§ 1148.5). Posting with certified mail at least 5 days before gives you possession only, no money judgment unless the tenant appears (§ 1148.5A).
  • The tenant needn’t file an answer before the hearing (§ 1148.6(C)), and possession is never tried to a jury (§ 1148.7).
  • If you win, ask the clerk for the writ, then give the 48-hour notice before it is executed (NOHM’s next form).
  • If the money you claim is $10,000 or less, the case can go on the small-claims docket; there an LLC or corporation can appear through an officer, member, manager or full-time employee (§§ 1148.14, 1751).
  • The court awards the winner a reasonable attorney fee (§ 1148.9).
  • Bring the lease, the notice and how you served it, and the rent ledger.

48-hour notice before the writ is executed Court

Tells the tenant you, your agent or an officer will return in at least 48 hours to restore you to possession under the writ.

When
You won, the clerk issued the writ, and the tenant is still there.
The law
Okla. Stat. tit. 12, § 1148.10A(A), (C)
Good to know
  • Ask for the writ after judgment (Okla. Stat. tit. 12, § 1148.10). A motion for a new trial (only within 3 days) doesn’t stop it.
  • Give this notice to the tenant in person, or post it in a conspicuous place at the premises (§ 1148.10A(A), (C)). Come back no sooner than 48 hours after, at the date and time in it.
  • You may call the sheriff or the city police to help (§ 1148.10A(E)). Never remove the tenant or their things by force yourself.
  • Within 2 days of the judgment (up to 7 if the court allows) the tenant may post a supersedeas bond and appeal; they must then pay rent into the court clerk’s office while the appeal runs (§ 1148.10A(F)).

Move-in.

Move-in condition checklist

The written record of the home’s condition when the tenant moves in, signed by both of you.

When
Give it at move-in, and keep the signed copy.
Good to know
  • Oklahoma doesn’t require a move-in checklist. It is good practice: walk through with the tenant, both sign it, and keep a copy for the deposit.
  • Required in writing at or before move-in: who manages the premises, who owns them (or their agent for service of process and notices), and, in the lease itself, prominently, who is entitled to accept service and notices (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 116(A)). Keep them current.
  • If the premises flooded within the past 5 years and you know it, say so prominently in the written lease (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 113a(A)).
  • If you know or have reason to know it was used to make methamphetamine, disclose it before the lease starts, unless it tested at or below 0.1 µg per 100 cm² (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 118(C)).
  • An agreement for the tenant to do repairs must be in a conspicuous writing separate from the lease (§ 118(B)); give the tenant your rules at signing (§ 126(A)(6)).
  • Built before 1978: give the federal lead-based paint disclosure and pamphlet.

During the lease.

Notice of entry

Tells the tenant when you’ll come in, and why.

When
At least 24 hours before you enter.
The law
Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 128(C)
Good to know
  • Give at least one day’s notice and enter only at reasonable times, except in an emergency or where notice is impracticable (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 128(C)).
  • In an emergency you may enter without consent (§ 128(B)). Don’t use entry to harass the tenant.
  • If a tenant refuses lawful entry you may seek an injunction or end the agreement (§ 128(E)); entering unlawfully lets the tenant recover damages (§ 124).

Notice of rent change

Changes a month-to-month tenant’s rent from a rent due date.

When
At least 30 days before the new rent starts.
Good to know
  • No Oklahoma statute sets notice for a rent increase. NOHM asks for 30 days, the notice that ends a month-to-month tenancy (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 111(A)), and starts the new rent on a due date.
  • During a fixed-term lease you can change the rent only if the lease allows it.
  • Oklahoma has no rent cap, and cities can’t regulate rents (Okla. Stat. tit. 11, § 14-101.1).

Move-out.

Security deposit: itemized deductions

The written list of what you kept from the deposit, and why, with the refund.

When
Within 45 days after the tenancy ends and you get the home back.
The law
Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 115(B)
Good to know
  • Return the balance, with this itemized statement, within 45 days after the tenancy ends, you get possession back, and the tenant asks for the deposit in writing (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 115(B)). If they don’t ask in writing within 6 months, it reverts to you.
  • Send it by return-receipt mail, signed for by someone of service age at the address, or hand it to the tenant if you can reasonably find them.
  • Keep the deposit in an escrow account at a federally insured bank in Oklahoma; misappropriating it is a crime (§ 115(A)). No interest is owed.
  • If you don’t follow § 115, the tenant can recover the deposit and any prepaid rent, and the winner gets attorney’s fees (§§ 115(E), 105(B)).
  • Oklahoma doesn’t cap deposits. If you sell, transfer the deposits and tell the tenants in writing who holds them (§ 115(C)).

Notice of property left behind

Lists what the tenant left, and how long you’ll hold it before you dispose of it.

When
After the tenancy ended and the tenant left things behind.
The law
Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 130(B)
Good to know
  • Send it by certified mail to the tenant’s last-known address for property that has an ascertainable or apparent value (Okla. Stat. tit. 41, § 130(B)). Property with no ascertainable value, and perishables, you may dispose of without notice (§ 130(A)).
  • The statute lets you specify the time; NOHM fills in 30 days, when property left with you is conclusively abandoned.
  • Store it safely and with reasonable care. Storage in the unit can’t cost more than its fair rental value; a commercial storage company’s actual charges can be passed on (§ 130(C)).
  • Use it only after the tenant abandoned or surrendered the unit, or was lawfully removed by eviction.
  • If a sole tenant died, follow § 130.1 instead (the person they designated, 30 days from the notice’s postmark).
