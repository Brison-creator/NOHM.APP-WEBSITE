Colorado 10-day demand to comply with the lease or move

Describes the violation and requires the tenant to comply within 10 days, or give the home back.

Updated November 7, 2026

Sign in on the web

The facts.

  • WhenThe tenant broke a material term of the lease.
  • The lawColo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-104(1)(e); Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-106; Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-108(1); Colo. Rev. Stat. § 2-4-108
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • Describe the violation specifically: the law requires it (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-106(1)). For conduct that disturbed others or a nuisance, describe the conduct and demand that it stop (§ 13-40-104(1)(j)).
  • NOHM gives 10 days, the residential notice. Only 5 days apply to an “exempt residential agreement” (a single-family home whose landlord owns five or fewer and whose lease says the 10-day notice doesn’t apply), and 3 days to employer-provided housing (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-104). A property with a federally backed mortgage or in a federal housing program needs 30 days under the CARES Act (court form JDF 99 A).
  • A second violation of the same condition after this demand allows a notice ending the tenancy in 10 days with no chance to cure (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-104(1)(e.5); court form JDF 99 B). A substantial violation (a violent or drug felony, endangering people or property) allows 3 days (§ 13-40-107.5). NOHM doesn’t draw those; the court’s forms are free.
  • A documented victim-survivor isn’t in unlawful detention if domestic violence, stalking or sexual misconduct caused or contributed to the violation (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-104(4)).
  • The law requires the date and time to deliver up the premises (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-106(1)); NOHM states 11:59 p.m. on the last day.
  • Write it in English, Spanish, or the tenant’s primary language if you know or should know it’s another (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-106(3)). The court posts JDF 99 A in many languages.
  • If the tenant has told you they are a victim-survivor of domestic violence, stalking or sexual misconduct, make every reasonable effort to serve them in person; after three failed tries, post it AND send it by certified mail or a courier with proof of delivery (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-108(2)).
  • NOHM’s calendar doesn’t know Frances Xavier Cabrini Day (the first Monday in October, a Colorado legal holiday): if the last day falls on it, move it a day later.
