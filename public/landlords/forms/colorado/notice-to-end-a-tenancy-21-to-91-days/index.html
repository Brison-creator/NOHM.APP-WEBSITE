Colorado notice to end a tenancy (21 to 91 days)

Ends a tenancy at the end of a period: 21 days for a month-to-month the just-cause law doesn’t cover; 90 days and a stated cause once it does.

At a glance.

  • WhenYou want a tenancy to end; after 12 months you need a cause the law allows.
  • The lawColo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-107(1)–(3); Colo. Rev. Stat. §§ 38-12-1301 to -1307; Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-108(1)
  • StageNotices before court

What to know.

  • For a tenancy the just-cause law doesn’t cover (the tenant there less than 12 months, an owner-occupied home of up to three units, and others in Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-1302): the notice must expire at the end of the period, served at least 21 days before for a month-to-month, 28 for a tenancy of six months to a year, 91 for a year or longer, and 3 for week-to-week (§ 13-40-107(2)). Court form JDF 99 B.
  • For a covered tenancy, end it only for a cause the statute allows. A no-fault cause (demolition or conversion, substantial repairs, the landlord or family moving in, a sale, refusal to sign a reasonable new lease, a history of late rent) needs at least 90 days’ notice ending with the lease term and a statement of the legal and factual basis (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-1303(3), (5)(b)). Court form JDF 99 C. NOHM gives 91 days once the tenant has been there 12 months.
  • A notice that doesn’t meet the just-cause law is a defense, and the case is dismissed (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-1306). A rent increase meant to get around it is barred (§ 38-12-1307).
  • Colorado’s court forms use no further demand after this notice: if the tenant stays, the eviction rests on it (court forms JDF 99 B and 99 C). Whether a court wants another demand is unsettled.
  • If the tenant has told you they are a victim-survivor of domestic violence, stalking or sexual misconduct, make every reasonable effort to serve them in person; after three failed tries, post it AND send it by certified mail or a courier with proof of delivery (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 13-40-108(2)).

The statute doesn’t set this notice’s exact words: check it before you serve one.
