What to know.

  • Maryland’s District Court uses the Judiciary’s forms: DC-CV-115 before a rent case, then DC-CV-082 (the court requires its carbonless multi-part form, from any District Court location), DC-CV-080 or DC-CV-085. Fill them in from this worksheet; this worksheet isn’t filed.
  • Each complaint is affirmed under the penalties of perjury: check every amount against the ledger.
  • A rental license is required in some counties and cities; where it is, the complaint must give it (Real Prop. § 8-406).
  • In a failure-to-pay-rent case anyone may represent you (Bus. Occ. & Prof. § 10-206(b)(1)). In a holdover or breach case a business may need a lawyer; the Judiciary says only that some entities must have one, and NOHM couldn’t confirm the rule. Ask the clerk.
  • Baltimore City, Montgomery County, Prince George’s County and Takoma Park have their own rules that may add to these.
