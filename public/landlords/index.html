Questions.

How much does NOHM cost for landlords?

NOHM is free to start: rentals you don’t enroll stay on Free. Financial Management is $7 a month per enrolled rental, and NOHM Autopilot is $19.99 a month per Autopilot rental with Financial Management included. Either way there’s one $9 account fee a month while any rental is on a paid plan. Walkthroughs are $79 each.

Can I put different rentals on different plans?

Yes. Choose a plan for each rental and change it any time in the app. 2 rentals on Autopilot and 1 on Financial Management is $9 + 2 × $19.99 + $7 = $55.98 a month.

Why do I need a card on file on Free?

It helps keep NOHM safe from fraud and lets you request service when something breaks. You’re not charged on Free unless you book or buy something.

Does NOHM take a cut of my rent?

No. Rent goes straight to your connected payout account. A bank (ACH) rent payment has a flat $7 fee, whatever the rent. You decide, lease by lease, whether your tenant pays it on top or you pay it from your payout.

What does NOHM Autopilot actually do?

It answers your tenants’ routine questions in the same conversation: when rent is due, what’s owed, how to pay. A repair message becomes a maintenance request, handled within the limit you set. Anything else, like pets, lease changes or money requests, comes to you, and you can review what it did and take over any time. It relists the unit after move-out and sends you a weekly summary. Emergency reports always come straight to you.

What is a NOHM Walkthrough?

A move-in or move-out condition report done in person by a trained local specialist. Every room is rated and photographed, both sides sign it, and it’s saved as a signed PDF. You can also do it yourself in the app for free.

Who screens my applicants?

Renters apply free. If they add screening, Certn runs it: Standard Screening ($55) or NOHM Ready ($75), which also shows how they compare with your published requirements. The renter buys it, you see the results they share, and you make the decision. If you want proof of income, ask for the NOHM Income Check: the applicant connects their bank through Plaid and you see their income against the rent. It is $20, on any plan, only if they connect. NOHM never asks for W-2s or bank statements, which can be faked. Learn more about Certn

Can I use my own lease?

Yes. Upload your lease as a PDF and you and your tenant e-sign it in the app. NOHM keeps a signing certificate with it.

How do deposits work at move-out?

Set the move-out date and NOHM tracks the deposit deadline. Itemize any deductions next to the move-in report, send the statement and mark the refund paid. Reminders go out before the deadline.

Can I see profit for each unit?

Yes. Money & Taxes shows income, costs and profit per rental, sorted into IRS Schedule E categories, with a year-end report you can email to your accountant.

Can I book a pro myself, without a tenant request?

Yes. Tap Request a Service, pick the unit and choose Standard, Express or NOHM NOW. Approve the estimate before work starts and follow your pro live.

What stays with the unit when a tenant moves out?

Everything. Repairs, condition reports and records are tied to the unit’s Home ID, so the next tenant and the next owner get the full history.

What if something goes wrong with a job?

Most things, like the quality of the work, are between you and your pro. NOHM steps in on no-shows, unsafe conduct and charges you didn’t approve. Learn more
