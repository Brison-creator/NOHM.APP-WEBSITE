Questions.

Can I manage more than one property?

Yes. Add as many single-family homes and multi-family buildings as you own or manage. Duplexes, fourplexes, condos and larger buildings are split into units, and every unit gets its own Home ID.

How do my tenants join?

Enter their phone number, rent and lease dates. NOHM texts them a code. They download the app, enter the code and they’re connected to their unit.

Can tenants book a pro on their own?

No. Tenants send a request and you decide. You can approve it, decline it with a reason, or set limits so smaller jobs go without waiting on you.

Can I book a pro myself, without a tenant request?

Yes. Tap Request a Service, pick the property and choose Standard, Express or NOHM Now. You can choose your pro or let NOHM match one, then follow the job live.

Do I approve the price first?

Always. The pro sends an estimate and nothing starts until you approve it. Extras need your OK too.

Who pays for the repair?

You do, as the owner. Jobs you approve are charged to your card on file. Tenants never pay NOHM for repairs.

Can I collect rent in NOHM?

Yes. Set the rent and due day for each tenant and collect it online in the app.

How do rental applications work?

Send the applicant a link. They complete the application on their phone, sign it and pay the $45 application fee. You get the full application and a screening report.

What stays with the unit when a tenant moves out?

Everything. The repair history is tied to the unit’s Home ID, not the tenant, so the next tenant and the next owner get the full record.

I also own my own home. Do I need two accounts?

No. Add a homeowner role, or even a pro role, to the same account and switch between them.

What if something goes wrong with a job?

Most things, like the quality of the work, are between you and your pro. NOHM steps in on no-shows, unsafe conduct and charges you didn’t approve. Learn more
