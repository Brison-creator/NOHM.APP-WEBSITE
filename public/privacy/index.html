A. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Identifiers and contact data (name, email, phone number, mailing address)

B. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Services
  • Authenticate users, verify accounts, reduce fraud, protect safety, and enforce policies
  • Deliver transactional and customer-care communications (verification, account notices, service updates, support responses)
  • Facilitate connections and communications between users and independent service providers, where applicable
  • Analyze performance, reliability, and usage
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

C. SMS/Text Messaging

By creating an account or providing your phone number, you consent to receive transactional and customer-care SMS/MMS messages from NOHM related to your account and service activity. These messages are strictly operational and may include:

  • Account verification — one-time passcodes to verify your identity (e.g., "Your verification code is 123456. Valid for 10 minutes.")
  • Service status updates — notifications when a professional accepts your request, arrives on site, or marks a job complete (e.g., "Pro accepted your request for 123 Main St. Track status in the app.")
  • Job completion notices — confirmation when work is finished (e.g., "Job at 123 Main St is marked complete.")
  • Support messages — responses to your help or support inquiries
  • Message frequency varies based on your account activity. Message and data rates may apply. NOHM does not send marketing or promotional text messages.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help or contact us at Admin@nohm.app. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchase or use of the Services. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We may disclose information:

  • To protect rights, safety, and security, investigate fraud/abuse, or enforce agreements
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets (with appropriate protections)
  • Opt-in data and consent for SMS messaging will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile phone numbers collected for transactional or customer care SMS will only be used to deliver such messages and will not be sold, licensed, or shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional use. Service providers who process SMS on our behalf are contractually prohibited from using opt-in data or mobile numbers for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
