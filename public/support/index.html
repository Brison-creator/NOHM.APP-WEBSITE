Getting Started

How do I create a NOHM account?

Download NOHM from the App Store or Google Play, tap Sign Up, enter your mobile number, and verify the one-time code we text you. Then add your service address — that address becomes your Home Identity Number (HIN).

What is a Home Identity Number (HIN)?

A HIN is a permanent record tied to your address. Every NOHM service visit — repair, inspection, install — is recorded under the HIN, so the home keeps its full service history even if owners or tenants change.

Do I need to be a homeowner to use NOHM?

No. Homeowners, landlords, property managers, and tenants can all use NOHM. Tenants can request work that their landlord has authorized; landlords can manage units and assign limited access.

Which areas does NOHM serve?

We are launching market by market. Email support@nohm.app to ask about coverage in your area.

Verification & Sign-In

I'm not receiving my SMS verification code.

Confirm the phone number you entered, check signal, and request a new code after 60 seconds. If you still don't receive it, reply HELP to any prior NOHM message or email support@nohm.app with your number so we can investigate carrier delivery.

How do I change my phone number or email?

Open Settings → Account in the app and tap the field you want to update. You'll be asked to verify the new number or email before the change takes effect.

What if I lose access to my phone?

Email support@nohm.app from the address on file. We will verify identity through additional checks before transferring the account to a new device.

Service Requests

How do I request a repair?

Tap Get Service on the home screen, pick a category (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, etc.), describe the issue, and confirm your address. NOHM dispatches a verified local professional and you'll see a confirmation within the 15-minute confirm window.

What does "GPS-Verified Arrival" mean?

Your pro must be within 500 feet of your address before the app can mark them as ARRIVED. This prevents "on my way" billing tricks and keeps the timeline honest.

What is the PIN for?

Work officially starts only after you enter your 4-digit PIN. The pro cannot begin billable work until you authorize it on-site. This protects you from unauthorized charges.

How do I cancel or reschedule a service?

Open the job in the app and tap Cancel or Reschedule. Cancellations before a pro accepts are free. Once a pro has been dispatched, a small trip fee may apply per the pro's policy.

Payments & Receipts

How do I add or update a payment method?

Open Settings → Payment Methods. NOHM uses Stripe for all card processing — we never store your full card number on our servers.

When am I charged?

You are charged after the pro marks the job complete and the invoice is finalized. You'll receive an in-app and email receipt for every charge.

How do refunds work?

If something is wrong with the work or the charge, open the job in the app and tap Report an Issue, or email support@nohm.app within 7 days. We work directly with you and the pro to resolve it.

Where can I find my receipts and invoices?

Open Activity → History to see every past job, invoice, and receipt — permanently stored on your HIN.

Privacy & Safety

Does NOHM sell my data?

No. NOHM does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. See our full Privacy Policy at nohm.app/privacy.

Are my phone number and address shared with the pro?

Pros see only what they need to do the job: your first name, the service address, and the job description. Phone numbers and emails are redacted from in-platform messages and routed through privacy-preserving relays where possible.

How are professionals screened?

Every pro applies through NOHM, passes a background check, and provides license/insurance documentation where required. See Terms § C.2 for the full screening disclosure.

What if I feel unsafe during a visit?

Your safety comes first. Call 911 immediately for emergencies. After the situation is safe, email support@nohm.app — we investigate every safety report and can remove pros from the network.

Notifications & Messages

What text messages will NOHM send me?

Only transactional messages: verification codes, service status updates, job-completion notices, and replies to support requests. NOHM does not send marketing or promotional texts.

How do I stop receiving SMS?

Reply STOP to any NOHM text to opt out of non-essential messages. Reply HELP for help. Standard message and data rates may apply.

How do I manage push notifications?

Open Settings → Notifications in the app. You can mute notifications by category (status updates, messages, receipts, etc.).

Technical Issues

The app keeps crashing or won't load.

First, force-close and reopen the app. If that doesn't help, update to the latest version from the App Store / Google Play and restart your device. Still seeing issues? Email support@nohm.app with your device model and OS version.

I can't see my address or the map looks wrong.

Make sure NOHM has Location permission set to While Using the App in your device settings. Then pull-to-refresh the home screen.

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Tap Settings → Send Feedback in the app, or email support@nohm.app. Screenshots or a short screen recording help us reproduce the problem quickly.

Account & Deletion

How do I delete my NOHM account?

Open Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app, or email support@nohm.app from your account email. We confirm by SMS and delete personal data within 30 days, retaining only the minimum required by law (for example, tax-related invoice records).

Can I download my data?

Yes. Email support@nohm.app from your account email and request a data export. We will deliver it within 30 days.
